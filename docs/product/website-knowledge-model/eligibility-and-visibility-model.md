# Eligibility and Visibility Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Principle

Visibility is a governed presentation decision for a declared audience and
purpose. It never changes canonical Review, lifecycle, authority, or publication state.

| Canonical/presentation condition | Website treatment | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| visible | eligible representation may be shown to the declared audience with status and limits | visible does not mean true, applicable, or published unless separately stated |
| hidden | representation is intentionally not shown for a governed reason | hiding does not delete, reject, or change canonical content |
| internal | limited to separately authorized internal purposes and audiences | cannot leak through search, counts, links, snippets, or errors |
| review pending | shown only where audience/purpose permits, prominently pending | not accepted, current, or publication eligible |
| not published | no public presentation; status remains explicit internally where authorized | repository presence or URL reachability does not publish it |
| deprecated | eligible historical/current display states discouraged use and rationale | deprecation is not deletion or supersession automatically |
| superseded | prior version remains traceable with explicit approved replacement | replacement does not rewrite historical meaning |

## Eligibility Inputs

Eligibility considers exact canonical and Representation versions, audience,
purpose, Knowledge/Scientific review, rights, privacy, security, sensitive
location/person context, publication decision, lifecycle, language review,
limitations, and unresolved issues. Website Alpha does not decide these inputs.

## Precedence

The most restrictive applicable canonical, rights, privacy, security, lifecycle,
or Publication condition controls visibility. A Website editorial preference
cannot override it. Program Administrator status is not universal visibility authority.

## Absence Semantics

Not displayed may mean hidden, internal, pending, not published, withdrawn,
ineligible, unavailable, or absent from the selected discovery scope. It must
not be presented as scientific nonexistence or negative evidence.

## Lifecycle Changes

Visibility reacts to approved canonical and publication decisions through
future separately governed integration. It does not originate deprecation,
supersession, withdrawal, retirement, or publication.
