# Composition Rules

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Admission Rules

1. Compose Pages from approved Knowledge Views, never directly from Canonical Objects.
2. Admit only Views eligible for the Page's purpose, audience, language, and publication context.
3. Identify exact canonical and representation versions and verify compatibility.
4. Preserve provenance, authority, Review State, publication state, limitations, and traceability.
5. Declare editorial grouping, ordering, summarization, and omission where they could affect interpretation.

## Permitted Composition

A Knowledge Page may contain Concept, Observation, Relationship, Investigation,
Source, Explainability, Review, and other approved Views where the Page contract
and source governance permit them. Reuse and cross-navigation are allowed without
copying or changing canonical meaning.

## Prohibitions

A Page must never:

- author or approve knowledge;
- change canonical meaning or identity;
- hide material provenance, authority, Review State, publication state, or limitations;
- create a Relationship from adjacency, sequence, co-occurrence, or navigation;
- infer applicability, causality, equivalence, ranking, Diagnosis, or Recommendation;
- merge incompatible versions or present historical content as current;
- convert visibility into publication approval; or
- create Runtime, workflow, editing, or decision behavior.

## Conflict Rule

When Views conflict or compatibility is unresolved, composition preserves their
distinct identities and states, discloses the limitation where eligible, and
defers resolution to the owning authority. Layout cannot resolve scientific or
governance disagreement.

## Failure Rule

Unknown provenance, version, authority, review, or publication status blocks any
composition purpose that depends on it. The missing state is never silently filled.
