# Knowledge View Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Definition

A Knowledge View is a governed representation of one or more Canonical Knowledge
Objects prepared for a declared presentation purpose and audience.

A Knowledge View preserves:

- canonical identity and semantic meaning;
- provenance and transformation history;
- authority and its scope;
- Review State and applicable conditions;
- publication state and visibility limits; and
- canonical and representation version identity.

## Identity Separation

A Knowledge View is not a Knowledge Object and never becomes one. It references
the exact canonical objects and versions it represents. Its own representation
identity records presentation purpose, audience, language, permitted omissions,
and fidelity review without replacing canonical identity.

## Reuse

An eligible Knowledge View may be reused across multiple Website pages and
sections. Reuse does not copy authority or create a new scientific version. Each
use retains the same reference, governance metadata, and limitations, while the
composition records its local purpose and placement.

## Compatibility

Views may be composed together only when their exact versions, meanings,
visibility, audience, and publication conditions are compatible. Incompatible or
unknown versions are not silently merged. A composition may disclose the conflict
or exclude an ineligible view; it may not resolve the conflict by inference.

## Existing View Types

Concept, Investigation, Observation, Source, Relationship, Explainability, and
Package Views remain governed by the
[Website Knowledge View Model](../website-knowledge-model/website-knowledge-view-model.md).
This composition model consumes those abstractions and does not redefine them.
