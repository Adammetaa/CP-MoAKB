# Traceability Preservation Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Required Trace

Every material element on a composed Page preserves a deterministic path:

> Page placement -> Section -> Knowledge View and Representation Version ->
> Canonical Object identity and version -> Canonical Knowledge provenance

Composition must never break, replace, or obscure this path.

## Traceability Record

Conceptually, each placement retains:

- Page and Section purpose;
- Knowledge View identity, type, and version;
- exact canonical object references and versions;
- provenance and transformation references;
- source and asserted Relationship references;
- authority and Review State;
- publication and visibility state;
- composition rationale, omissions, and limitations; and
- supersession or deprecation context.

## Summaries and Multi-view Sections

A summary remains traceable to every View supporting its material content. A
multi-view Section keeps each View's identity distinct; a shared heading does not
collapse provenance or create a combined Claim. Material discrepancies remain visible.

## Navigation

Navigation may follow canonical asserted Relationships or identified editorial
links. The kind and direction remain distinguishable. A navigation path does not
become scientific evidence, an inferred Relationship, or authority transfer.

## Historical Integrity

Superseded and deprecated Views retain their historical trace and applicable
status. Replacement links add context without rewriting the identity, meaning,
or provenance of prior content.
