# Investigation Blueprint Purpose and Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Product Purpose

The blueprint gives later Product, Knowledge, UX, and Runtime sprints a shared,
implementation-neutral account of the smallest safe SPA investigation. It
defines responsibilities, readiness, human handoffs, permissible outputs, and
epistemic boundaries while leaving implementation choices open.

## Product Questions Answered

An Investigation Case is a governed product container for one bounded inquiry.
A case may open with limited attributable information, but investigation begins
only when subject, reporter, responsible SPA, reported concern, and uncertainty
can be traced safely. Noncritical information may remain explicitly unknown,
unavailable, or not applicable. An SPA moves by recording, distinguishing,
asking, collecting, reviewing, comparing, and escalating through authored human
acts rather than system transitions.

A case must pause the affected activity when required identity, safety,
authority, context, or evidence is inadequate. Agronomist or specialist review
is required when SPA authority, uncertainty, risk, conflict, or scientific
complexity demands it. Stage-visible outputs must disclose provenance,
provisional status, missing information, and review standing.

## Inclusion Boundary

The blueprint covers case intake, Information State review, Observation capture,
gap identification, manual question use, evidence collection and review,
provisional candidate authoring, differential comparison, escalation, reviewed
findings, Management Options eligibility, Decision, Action, follow-up, Outcome,
visibility, and minimum MVP scope.

## Exclusion Boundary

It does not establish Diagnosis, choose a Hypothesis Candidate, accept Evidence
automatically, design Management Options, recommend pesticide or treatment,
make a user Decision, or infer an Outcome. It creates no production Question
Bank record, crop dataset, workflow engine, screen, form, or technical contract.

## Management Options Boundary

Management Options may become eligible for future governed consideration only
after minimum readiness, evidence review, differential comparison, required
human review, uncertainty disclosure, and applicable regulatory and safety
checks. Eligibility does not create an option. An option is not a Recommendation,
Decision, or Action. This sprint defines no active ingredient, Mode of Action,
product, rate, program, resistance strategy, or application instruction.

## Product Compatibility

The same blueprint supports phone and chat conversations, field visits, image
submission, remote review, and Agronomist consultation. A future Rice Vertical
Slice may specialize terminology and governed knowledge while preserving the
crop-independent core. Other governed vertical slices may extend the same model,
making the blueprint multi-crop extensible without introducing crop-specific
structure. Thai-first use must retain original wording, translation provenance,
uncertainty, and technical alignment.

## Change Control

Later designs may implement this blueprint only under separate authority. No
implementation may reinterpret stage descriptions as automatic transitions or
weaken the governing Knowledge Architecture.
