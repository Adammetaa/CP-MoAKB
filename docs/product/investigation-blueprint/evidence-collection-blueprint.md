# Evidence Collection Blueprint

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Core Boundary

Collection begins from an explicit Evidence Need. Collected material is not
automatically an Evidence Object. Quality concerns whether material is usable
and well characterized; relevance concerns whether separately reviewed material
bears on a precise question, candidate, or criterion. High quality does not
guarantee relevance, and relevant material may retain serious quality limitations.

## Visual and Specimen Categories

| Category | Why and question helped | Minimum context | Limitations and quality concerns | Privacy, sensitivity, and review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| close image | inspect bounded morphology or origin | subject, structure, time, scale, angle, source, capture method | narrow context, blur, lighting, compression, misleading color | remove unnecessary identifiers; SPA quality review and qualified relevance review |
| whole-plant image | relate a feature to plant context | plant identity basis, date, site context, scale, photographer | occlusion, selected plant, weak fine detail | location and people may be sensitive; review selection bias |
| field-distribution image | examine spatial arrangement | field bounds, direction, capture points, date, method | perspective distortion and incomplete coverage | precise field location is sensitive; Agronomist review may be needed |
| video | examine movement, sequence, or broader context | source, continuous time, location, narration attribution, transformations | editing, motion blur, missing scale, large irrelevant content | voices and faces require protection; review provenance and relevant segments |
| specimen | permit closer observation or specialist examination | collector, subject, place, time, method, condition, custody | degradation, contamination, nonrepresentative selection | biosafety, transport, ownership, and custody review required |

## Quantitative and Context Records

| Category | Why and question helped | Minimum context | Limitations and quality concerns | Privacy, sensitivity, and review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| count | bound frequency within a sampled context | counted entity, area, method, time, observer, exclusions | sampling bias, double count, detection limit | field sensitivity; method and relevance review |
| measurement | characterize a bounded property | value, unit, instrument, calibration or method, time, subject | precision, calibration, transcription, method mismatch | review method fitness and uncertainty |
| crop-stage record | clarify developmental context | identity basis, stage terminology, observer/source, date | terminology mismatch and uncertain identity | terminology and Agronomist review where material |
| management-history record | establish attributable prior events | actor/source, event, date, location, scope | recall error, incomplete sequence, attribution bias | commercial and personal sensitivity; do not infer cause |
| pesticide-use record | clarify reported prior use or safety context | exact source, product record as supplied, timing, place, actor | recall, label ambiguity, incomplete application context | high regulatory/commercial sensitivity; qualified review; no advice derived automatically |
| fertilizer-use record | clarify reported inputs | source, material as recorded, amount if known, timing, place | unit, product, and recall uncertainty | commercial sensitivity; qualified contextual review |
| irrigation record | understand water timing and condition | source or method, time, area, water event description | uneven distribution, missing measurements, recall | site sensitivity; distinguish report from measurement |
| weather record | establish bounded environmental context | authority/source, location resolution, time range, variables | station distance, interpolation, missing data | source licensing and location sensitivity; relevance review |
| field-location record | establish bounded site relationship | source, precision, date, coordinate or descriptive basis | stale or imprecise location, boundary ambiguity | precise locations require restricted handling and purpose review |

## Human, Laboratory, and Authority Sources

| Category | Why and question helped | Minimum context | Limitations and quality concerns | Privacy, sensitivity, and review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| farmer report | preserve experience, timing, and management context | reporter, language, date, channel, scope, uncertainty | recall, terminology, perspective, second-hand content | consent and personal/commercial privacy; retain as reported |
| SPA direct observation | document attributable field or remote capture | SPA observer, method, time, place, subject, limitations | observer and access limits; remote media constraints | supervisory or Agronomist review as appropriate |
| Agronomist observation | obtain qualified direct or remote account | reviewer identity/role, method, time, material examined, limitations | expertise scope and remote limitations | conflict-of-interest and role review |
| laboratory result | request bounded analysis relevant to a gap | laboratory/source, specimen custody, method, result, units, limits, date | sampling, method validity, detection limits, interpretation boundary | sensitive identity and regulatory implications; qualified scientific review |
| external authority source | clarify governed scientific, safety, or regulatory context | exact authority, title, version/date, jurisdiction, retrieval, relevant passage | outdated, wrong jurisdiction, secondary interpretation, license limits | Knowledge and Governance review; source does not decide the case |

## Collection Review

For every category, reviewers distinguish nomination, custody, quality review,
relevance assessment, and Evidence Object governance. They record missing
context, transformations, conflicts, privacy limits, and whether recollection is
safe or feasible. Absence of requested material never rejects a candidate automatically.
