# Human Review and Escalation Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Role Principle

Roles are conceptual responsibilities assigned to accountable humans for a
bounded case. They are not permanent organizational titles, identities, access
roles, or technical permissions. Assignment records expertise, authority,
conflicts, scope, and review purpose.

## Review Responsibilities

| Role | May observe and author | May challenge, approve, or reject | Clarification, unresolved, and publication eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPA reviewer | record attributed SPA observations, intake, gaps, question instances, needs, provisional candidates, and case findings within authority | challenge case content; approve routine investigation work only for stated SPA purpose; reject inadequate intake within authority | return for clarification and mark unresolved; cannot alone authorize scientific or governance publication eligibility unless separately assigned |
| Agronomist reviewer | author qualified observations, candidate analysis, comparison criteria, comments, and reviewed findings within expertise | challenge candidates and evidence roles; approve or reject agronomic review conclusions for bounded purpose | request evidence, return work, mark unresolved; contributes to but does not automatically grant publication eligibility |
| Scientific reviewer | author specialist assessment of methods, laboratory results, scientific identity, or evidence limitations | challenge, approve, or reject scientific interpretations within declared expertise | request clarification, preserve uncertainty, and support eligibility when scientific review is required |
| Knowledge reviewer | author reviews of terminology, sources, knowledge references, provenance, and knowledge-boundary use | challenge or reject unsupported knowledge use; approve knowledge conformance for stated purpose | return for citation or terminology clarification; may support knowledge publication eligibility under existing authority |
| Governance reviewer | author governance findings, authority decisions, conflicts, and boundary dispositions | approve or reject governance conformance; never substitutes for missing scientific expertise | may return, mark unresolved, escalate, or authorize publication eligibility only where existing governance grants that authority |

No role may convert Observation to Diagnosis, need to Evidence, candidate to
truth, finding to Claim, option to Recommendation, or review approval to publication.

## Escalation Situations

| Situation | Required response boundary |
|---|---|
| insufficient evidence | identify precise gaps and needs; pause unsupported findings |
| conflicting evidence | preserve all material accounts and request qualified comparison |
| high uncertainty | disclose uncertainty and seek Agronomist or specialist review |
| possible crop-safety risk | pause affected advice and escalate to qualified authority |
| possible human-safety risk | prioritize safety handoff and avoid unauthorized instruction |
| suspected regulatory issue | route to appropriate Governance or qualified regulatory authority |
| suspected resistance | preserve as provisional and seek qualified scientific and governance review |
| unusual symptom pattern | request broader evidence and Agronomist or Scientific review |
| unknown organism | avoid identity inference and request qualified identification basis |
| unresolved crop identity | limit dependent work and seek clarification or specialist review |
| unresolved growth stage | disclose limitation and pause stage-dependent comparison where material |
| prior treatment failure | record exact report and context; do not infer resistance or recommend escalation of treatment |
| repeated field failure | distinguish cases, patterns, and provenance; seek Agronomist and possibly Scientific review |
| pesticide recommendation request | do not answer under this blueprint; route only to separately authorized future governance |
| request beyond SPA authority | record the boundary and assign the appropriate accountable reviewer |

## Review Outcomes

A reviewer may approve for a stated investigative purpose, reject with criteria,
return for clarification, challenge content, or mark it unresolved. Human review
is mandatory before a finding is called reviewed. Silence, elapsed time,
completion, majority, or interface state is not approval.

## Escalation Record

Each escalation preserves initiator, reason, risk, affected activity, relevant
material, requested expertise, assigned reviewer, conflicts, urgency basis,
communications, response, unresolved issues, and disposition. These are
conceptual audit responsibilities, not a ticket schema.
