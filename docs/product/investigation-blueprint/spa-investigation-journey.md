# SPA Investigation Journey

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Journey Principle

The SPA is the primary coordinator of a traceable field investigation, not an
automatic diagnostic endpoint. The SPA records sources faithfully, discloses
uncertainty, works within authority, and escalates when the case exceeds that authority.

## Governed Journey

1. **Receive report:** capture the channel, reporter, bounded concern, urgency,
   and safety signals without rewriting the report as fact.
2. **Acknowledge uncertainty:** state what is reported, missing, conflicting, or
   pending review and set expectations about human investigation.
3. **Record what was reported:** preserve the reporter's meaning, language,
   context, and attribution.
4. **Separate report from direct observation:** create a direct Observation only
   when an attributable human or instrument actually captures one.
5. **Identify missing information:** assign explicit Information States and
   Information Gaps without manufacturing negative findings.
6. **Ask the next appropriate question:** manually consider a governed Question
   Pattern, adapt a Question Instance, or skip it with reason.
7. **Request or capture evidence:** link a precise Evidence Need to suitable
   material requests while preserving consent, safety, and context.
8. **Review evidence quality:** assess legibility, completeness, provenance,
   method, and limitations separately from relevance.
9. **Consult governed knowledge:** use exact references to inform questions and
   comparison, never to auto-generate a conclusion.
10. **Author provisional candidates:** explicitly state rationale, limitations,
    and alternatives; do not present a candidate as Diagnosis.
11. **Compare alternatives:** use explained criteria and supporting,
    challenging, missing, conflicting, or irrelevant evidence roles.
12. **Escalate when needed:** request Agronomist, Scientific, Knowledge, or
    Governance review for risk, uncertainty, conflict, authority, or content needs.
13. **Present reviewed findings:** show only the bounded reviewed result with
    uncertainty, unresolved issues, and review provenance.
14. **Present eligible Management Options:** only where separate future authority
    and eligibility checks permit; never convert them to Recommendations.
15. **Record farmer or user Decision:** preserve who decided, basis, alternatives,
    uncertainty, and timing separately from the options shown.
16. **Record Action:** describe what was actually done, by whom, where, and when;
    do not infer it from a Decision.
17. **Schedule follow-up:** state the purpose, responsible human, timing basis,
    safety considerations, and unresolved questions.
18. **Record Outcome:** capture a bounded later report or Observation without
    inferring attribution, efficacy, or causality.

## Channel Compatibility

Phone and chat require careful attribution and may limit direct Observation.
Field visits allow direct capture but still require method and context. Image
submission supports review only within capture limitations. Remote review must
preserve what the reviewer could and could not examine. Agronomist consultation
records a distinct reviewer contribution, not an invisible SPA conclusion.

## Pause and Resume

At any point, the SPA may pause affected work for missing safety information,
unresolved identity, inadequate evidence, contradictory accounts, unavailable
authority, or requested activity outside scope. Resumption is an explicit human
decision with its new basis; it is not caused by completion of a field or upload.
