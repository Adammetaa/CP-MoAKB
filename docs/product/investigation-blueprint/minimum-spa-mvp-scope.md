# Minimum SPA MVP Scope

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## MVP Objective

The smallest safe SPA product slice supports a human-led, incomplete-information
investigation through a reviewed finding and follow-up. It proves traceable
coordination and review, not automation, diagnosis, or recommendation.

## Critical Path

The recommended minimum includes:

1. manual Case Intake with reporter, observer, responsibility, uncertainty,
   urgency, safety, crop, site, time, and reported concern context;
2. manual Observation recording distinct from reported information;
3. explicit Information State recording, including missing and conflicting states;
4. visible Information Gaps with reason and blocking relevance;
5. manual Question Pattern consideration and Question Instance authoring;
6. response capture with uncertainty, unavailable, skipped, and contradictory states;
7. image and evidence attachment with provenance and quality limitations;
8. Evidence Need tracking distinct from Evidence Object review;
9. manual Hypothesis Candidate authoring with rationale and alternatives;
10. manual Differential Comparison using explained criteria and evidence roles;
11. mandatory Agronomist Review for the MVP reviewed-finding path;
12. a reviewed Investigation Finding with limits and unresolved issues; and
13. follow-up recording with responsible human, purpose, and timing basis.

Every step is a human act. The MVP may allow safe work to pause, return, remain
unresolved, close, or reopen without manufacturing completion.

## Minimum Safety and Governance Controls

The MVP must preserve role attribution, source versus observer distinction,
provisional labels, language provenance, sensitive-information handling,
required escalation, evidence quality-versus-relevance separation, adverse and
missing evidence, immutable review history in principle, and explicit absence of
diagnostic or pesticide authority.

## Deferred Outside the Critical Path

- image diagnosis and automatic Observation extraction;
- automatic Question Pattern selection or follow-up branching;
- automatic evidence acceptance or sufficiency decisions;
- automatic Hypothesis Candidate generation, scoring, ranking, or selection;
- automatic Diagnosis;
- pesticide, product, treatment, or resistance-strategy Recommendation;
- autonomous Decision or Action;
- self-learning from unreviewed cases;
- production Question Bank or crop-specific knowledge population;
- Management Options authoring beyond the eligibility boundary;
- outcome prediction and automatic causal or efficacy interpretation.

These are explicit deferrals, not implied roadmap approval.

## MVP Acceptance Questions

A future product review should verify that an SPA can preserve an incomplete
report, distinguish direct Observation, expose gaps, choose questions manually,
attach contextual material, author competing provisional candidates, obtain
Agronomist review, communicate a bounded reviewed finding, and schedule
follow-up—while the product never suggests a diagnosis or pesticide action.

## Implementation Boundary

This document defines product scope only. It specifies no screen, component,
form, API, database, storage, permission implementation, sequencing engine, or
technical acceptance test.
