# Investigation Blueprint Pattern Examples

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Use of Examples

These fictional examples illustrate architecture only. They are not production
case records, Question Bank entries, crop datasets, diagnoses, recommendations,
forms, screens, or executable workflows.

## Generic Incomplete Field Report

**Case received:** an SPA receives an attributable phone report and opens a
bounded case. The channel limits what can be directly observed.

**Reported information:** leaves appear damaged across part of the field.

The statement remains reported information in the reporter's language. It is
not treated as verified distribution, severity, or cause.

**Observation:** linear translucent feature observed on a leaf blade.

The Observation identifies the observer, capture context, plant structure,
time, method, and limitations. It does not become Diagnosis.

**Information Gap:** lesion initiation point not recorded.

The gap remains `not recorded`, not `not observed`, and does not assert absence.

**Question Pattern consideration:** the SPA manually considers a reviewed
localization pattern under the Question Bank Architecture. Applicability does
not select the pattern.

**Question Instance:** where on the leaf did the feature first become visible?

The SPA adapts and reviews this case-specific instance. The respondent may state
that the answer is unknown, not recorded, unavailable, or not applicable.

**Evidence Need:** close image showing the lesion origin and relationship to leaf veins.

The need explains relevant material; it is not the image or an Evidence Object.
If an image arrives, capture context, quality, privacy, and relevance are reviewed separately.

**Hypothesis Candidates:** several provisional alternatives remain under
consideration. Each is explicitly authored with rationale, basis, challenges,
missing evidence, and no implied order.

**Differential Comparison:** evidence is compared using explained criteria,
including supporting, challenging, conflicting, missing, and irrelevant roles,
without selecting a diagnosis or using a score.

**Human Review:** a qualified reviewer records what is supported, challenged,
and unresolved, including image limitations and the remaining location gap.

**Finding:** available evidence is insufficient for a confirmed diagnosis.

This is a bounded reviewed Investigation Finding, not a Claim, Diagnosis,
Recommendation, or Decision. Follow-up may request a safer or clearer view. No
pesticide, product, treatment rate, or Management Option is supplied.

## Remote-to-field Escalation

An image submission is too blurred to assess the authored criterion. Quality is
poor, while relevance remains a separate unresolved question. The SPA records
the limitation, does not reject any candidate, and requests a field observation
or Agronomist review. Upload completion does not advance the case automatically.

## Explicit Non-example

A product must not interpret the linear feature, rank a named condition first,
display a confidence score, offer a pesticide, infer that the user chose it,
record an Action from that supposed choice, or claim an Outcome. Each would
collapse a protected layer and exceeds this blueprint.
