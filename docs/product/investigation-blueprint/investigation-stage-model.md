# Investigation Stage Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Interpretation

Stages are governed product descriptions, not executable states or a mandatory
linear workflow. Entry and exit are explicit human decisions with traceable
bases. A case may pause, revisit prior work, or move to several possible stages.

## Stages 1–6

| Stage | Purpose and entry condition | Permitted / prohibited activities | Human role, exits, and blocks | Visible outputs and audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Received | Preserve a new attributable report; entry is receipt through an authorized channel. | Record source, urgency, safety, and uncertainty; do not diagnose or rewrite as Observation. | Intake SPA; exit to intake, rejection, or withdrawal; block unsafe or unattributable handling. | Receipt summary and limits; audit channel, time, source, custodian, and disposition. |
| Intake in progress | Establish case bounds; entry is human acceptance for intake. | Clarify context and consent; do not claim investigation readiness. | Responsible SPA; exit to incomplete, observation review, rejection, or withdrawal; block missing minimum opening basis. | Intake summary, known and missing information; audit authorship and changes. |
| Information incomplete | Make material gaps explicit; entry is reviewed insufficiency for the next purpose. | Ask neutral questions, record unavailable or not applicable states; do not map blanks to “no.” | SPA, with reviewer as needed; exit to intake, observation review, evidence collection, or withdrawal; block safety-critical gaps. | Gap list, unanswered/skipped questions; audit states, reasons, and attempts. |
| Observation review | Separate reported, directly observed, and measured accounts; entry is available observation material. | Review provenance, context, vocabulary, and limitations; do not convert reports or images automatically. | SPA or Agronomist reviewer; exit to evidence collection, investigation, or incomplete; block ambiguous provenance or unsafe capture. | Recorded Observations and limitations; audit observer, method, context, and review. |
| Evidence collection | Seek material for explicit Evidence Needs; entry is an authored need and safe collection basis. | Request or attach material and assess quality; do not accept relevance automatically. | SPA coordinates, qualified reviewer assesses; exit to investigation, incomplete, expert review, or withdrawal; block privacy, safety, consent, or unusable context. | Evidence inventory, needs, quality concerns; audit custody, source, transformations, and review. |
| Investigation in progress | Organize gaps, questions, evidence, and knowledge; entry is sufficient readiness for bounded inquiry. | Author provisional candidates and criteria; do not diagnose, rank, or recommend. | SPA with Agronomist access; exit to evidence collection, differential review, expert review, or incomplete; block insufficient authority or critical evidence. | Provisional candidates, unresolved issues, evidence roles; audit every authorship and basis. |

## Stages 7–12

| Stage | Purpose and entry condition | Permitted / prohibited activities | Human role, exits, and blocks | Visible outputs and audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Differential review | Compare two or more plausible candidates where appropriate; entry is explicit candidates and criteria. | Record support, challenge, conflict, missing, and irrelevant evidence; do not score or select automatically. | Agronomist or qualified reviewer; exit to investigation, evidence collection, expert review, or reviewed; block hidden criteria or missing alternatives. | Marked provisional comparison; audit criteria, adverse evidence, comments, and status. |
| Expert review required | Route work exceeding current authority; entry is recorded escalation reason. | Clarify, challenge, request evidence, or mark unresolved; do not bypass expertise or imply approval. | Required Agronomist, Scientific, Knowledge, or Governance reviewer; exit to prior stage, reviewed, withdrawn, or rejected; block unavailable required reviewer or unresolved safety risk. | Escalation reason and reviewer-only material as authorized; audit assignment, conflict, response, and rationale. |
| Reviewed | Record bounded human-reviewed findings; entry is completion of required reviews. | Approve for stated purpose, reject, return, or mark unresolved; do not promote Finding to Claim or Diagnosis. | Authorized reviewer; exit to investigation, expert review, options eligibility, follow-up, or closure; block unresolved mandatory review. | Reviewed finding, limits, dissent, unresolved issues; audit decision scope and authority. |
| Management-options eligible | Record eligibility for future governed options; entry is readiness, evidence, differential, review, uncertainty, safety, and regulatory checks. | Consider separately authorized options; do not design pesticide content or call an option a Recommendation. | Authorized human under future governance; exit to decision, review, follow-up, or closure; block failed checks or absent authority. | Eligibility and disclosures, not an automatic option; audit every prerequisite and reviewer. |
| Decision recorded | Preserve the user's explicit choice; entry is an attributable decision. | Record chosen, declined, or deferred path and basis; do not infer action. | Farmer, user, or authorized decision-maker with SPA recording; exit to action, follow-up, or closure; block unclear authority or consent. | Decision record with options and uncertainty; audit actor, timing, basis, and communication. |
| Action recorded | Preserve what was actually done; entry is an attributable action report or observation. | Record action context and deviations; do not infer Outcome or efficacy. | SPA or attributable actor; exit to follow-up, outcome, or closure; block safety, regulatory, or provenance concerns. | Action record and limitations; audit actor, time, place, source, and review. |

## Stages 13–18

| Stage | Purpose and entry condition | Permitted / prohibited activities | Human role, exits, and blocks | Visible outputs and audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Follow-up required | Preserve need for later information or review; entry is an explicit follow-up purpose. | Schedule and request bounded follow-up; do not assume compliance or outcome. | Responsible SPA or reviewer; exit to observation review, evidence collection, investigation, outcome, or closure; block absent responsibility or unsafe request. | Follow-up requirement and open issues; audit reason, owner, timing, attempts, and changes. |
| Outcome recorded | Capture a bounded later result; entry is an attributable report, Observation, or measurement. | Record context and compare descriptively where authorized; do not infer causality or efficacy. | SPA with reviewer as needed; exit to follow-up, reopened, or closed; block missing provenance or unsafe interpretation. | Outcome record, uncertainty, and limits; audit source, method, time, and linkage. |
| Closed | End current authorized work; entry is a human closure decision with reason. | Preserve summary and unresolved matters; do not certify Diagnosis, completeness, or success. | Responsible SPA or authorized reviewer; exit only to reopened; block missing audit, safety handoff, or required disclosure. | Closure summary and retained provisional markings; audit reason, authority, and open risks. |
| Reopened | Resume a closed case on new basis; entry is an authorized reopening decision. | Review new material and prior assumptions; do not erase history or resume automatically. | Responsible SPA and required reviewers; exit to any appropriate active stage; block absent new basis or authority. | Reopening reason and prior closure; audit initiator, evidence, scope, and changes. |
| Withdrawn | End participation or inquiry without adjudicating content; entry is an authorized withdrawal. | Record reason and required safety handoff; do not treat withdrawal as rejection or resolution. | Responsible SPA or authorized party; exit to reopened only under governance; block retention, consent, or safety obligations. | Restricted withdrawal summary as appropriate; audit authority, reason, and retained duties. |
| Rejected | Record that the matter or proposed content cannot be accepted for stated purpose; entry is an authorized reviewed rejection. | State failed criteria and possible clarification route; do not erase record or diagnose. | Authorized reviewer or governance role; exit to intake or reopened only on new basis; block unexplained or unauthorized rejection. | Scoped rejection and rationale; audit criteria, reviewer, dissent, and appeal basis. |

## Cross-stage Controls

All stages preserve missing information, provisional status, role authority,
privacy, language, provenance, review, conflicts, and history. Visible outputs
are filtered by the [Output and Visibility Model](investigation-output-and-visibility-model.md),
not merely by stage name.
