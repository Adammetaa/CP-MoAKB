# Hypothesis and Differential Workspace

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Product Purpose

The conceptual workspace helps humans preserve and compare provisional
Hypothesis Candidates without turning the product into a diagnosis engine. It
defines information responsibilities, not UI components, screens, database
structures, calculations, or interaction behavior.

## Candidate Responsibilities

Each candidate is explicitly authored and includes a bounded explanatory
proposition, author, time, Observation and Knowledge basis, rationale,
limitations, relevant Evidence Needs, challenges, alternatives, unresolved
issues, and Review State. Candidates may coexist, be challenged, remain
unresolved, be revised, or be withdrawn with history preserved.

No image, term match, missing value, rule, model, or prior case creates a
candidate. Candidate order, color, count, completeness, or review activity must
not communicate rank or truth.

## Differential Responsibilities

The conceptual view supports:

- a candidate list with no implied order;
- candidate rationale and explicit knowledge basis;
- separately assessed supporting evidence;
- challenging evidence and reviewer objections;
- missing evidence and open Evidence Needs;
- conflicting evidence with all material accounts retained;
- explained Comparison Criteria applicable across candidates;
- reviewer comments, dissent, and scope;
- unresolved issues and next human-review needs; and
- independent Review State for candidates, criteria, evidence roles, and comparison.

Evidence may support, challenge, conflict with, or remain irrelevant to a
candidate or criterion. These roles are scoped human assessments. Support is not
proof; challenge is not automatic rejection; irrelevance is not poor quality;
absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

## Comparison Method Boundary

Reviewers state why each criterion matters, which governed knowledge supports
its use, what evidence bears on it, and what remains unresolved. Comparison may
describe similarities and differences but defines no score, weight, probability,
ranking, threshold, or winner. A single remaining candidate is still not Diagnosis.

## Visibility and Review

Provisional status must be prominent wherever candidates appear. Reviewers see
adverse and missing material, not only support. SPA-visible candidate content
depends on authority and review stage; Service Provider or external visibility
may be narrower. A reviewed Investigation Finding summarizes bounded results
without becoming Claim or Diagnosis.

## Escalation

The SPA seeks qualified review when candidates involve high uncertainty, safety,
regulation, suspected resistance, unusual patterns, unknown organisms,
unresolved crop identity or growth stage, prior treatment failure, repeated
field failure, or expertise beyond SPA authority.
