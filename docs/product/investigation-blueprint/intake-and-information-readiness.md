# Intake and Information Readiness

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Readiness Principle

Readiness is a human-reviewed assessment for a specific activity, not a global
completion score. Missing information may remain explicit without blocking
unrelated safe work. Safety, authority, consent, and subject ambiguity may block
the affected activity even when many details are present.

## Readiness Categories

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Required to open a case | attributable intake source or reporter; bounded or explicitly unresolved subject; reported concern; responsible intake owner; initial urgency and safety screen; lawful basis for handling necessary information |
| Required to begin investigation | case scope; reporter-versus-observer distinction; sufficient crop and site context for the bounded inquiry; at least one recorded Observation or explicit plan to obtain one; initial Information States and Gaps; responsible SPA; no unresolved critical safety block |
| Required before differential comparison | explicit candidates where appropriate; observation and knowledge basis; explained criteria; relevant Evidence Needs and reviewed material; visible missing, challenging, and conflicting evidence |
| Required before expert review | review question, case scope, relevant records, uncertainty, escalation reason, safety or regulatory flags, and a traceable package adequate for the requested expertise |
| Optional but useful | noncritical history, additional images, fuller environmental context, prior comparison records, and contact preferences that improve inquiry without creating false completeness |
| Sensitive information | personal, precise location, commercial, treatment, safety, custody, or other protected context handled only for authorized purpose and visibility |
| Unavailable information | relevant information that cannot currently be accessed or obtained; it remains unavailable, not negative |
| Not applicable information | a requirement reviewed as inapplicable to the bounded purpose with rationale; it is not omitted or unknown |

## Intake Information Considerations

| Information | Readiness use and boundary |
|---|---|
| crop identity | required when the investigation depends on it; unresolved identity must be explicit and may require escalation |
| crop growth stage | important for interpretation and differential review; may be unknown initially |
| field or site | bounded context; precise location may be sensitive |
| date and time | anchors report, Observation, capture, and progression; uncertainty is allowed |
| reporter | identifies who communicated information and under what channel |
| observer | kept distinct from reporter and reviewer |
| reported problem | retained in the reporter's terms without diagnostic normalization |
| affected plant structure | supports localization; unknown does not mean unaffected |
| approximate distribution | bounded reported or observed arrangement, not prevalence automatically |
| onset and progression | attributable temporal account; absence of history is not no progression |
| management history | contextual record, not an attribution of cause or efficacy |
| recent pesticide or fertilizer use | potentially sensitive context requiring exact source; never treatment advice |
| irrigation or water condition | reported, observed, or measured basis must be distinguished |
| weather context | exact source and time bounds; does not establish causality |
| image availability | availability only; image quality and relevance are separate reviews |
| specimen availability | availability and custody only; capture does not create Evidence |
| prior actions | attributable acts, distinct from Decisions and Outcomes |
| urgency | human triage statement with basis, not severity inference |
| safety concern | prompts an authorized safety pause or escalation, not a diagnosis |

## Stop and Request More Information

The SPA pauses the affected investigation activity when minimum case subject,
crop context, observation basis, safety context, evidence provenance, reviewer
authority, or comparison basis is insufficient for that activity. The SPA may
continue safe independent tasks and must record the blocking gap, question or
need, attempts, unavailable states, escalation, and reconsideration basis.

## No Form Definition

This blueprint does not prescribe fields, required-field markers, screen order,
validation, conditional display, or submission logic. Future forms must preserve
the readiness categories and must not coerce missing information into certainty.
