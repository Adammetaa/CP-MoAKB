# Delivery Plan Purpose and Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Objective

The plan identifies the smallest operational slice that creates SPA value, the
capabilities that must travel together, safe deferrals, prerequisites, knowledge
and review readiness, field validation evidence, mandatory stops, and learning
required before expansion.

## Delivery Principle

An increment is accepted because users can perform a bounded, governed job and
validation evidence supports expansion—not because implementation is complete.
Each increment must work independently within its scope, preserve incomplete and
unresolved states, expose provenance, and have a safe rollback posture.

## Inclusion Boundary

The plan covers product delivery sequencing, readiness categories, knowledge and
human capabilities, parallel preparation, pilot rounds, case sampling,
measurement candidates, observable success and failure evidence, stop criteria,
conceptual rollback, debriefs, Owner approval, deferred scope, and architecture risks.

## Exclusion Boundary

It does not design implementation architecture, technical components, UI,
permissions, synchronization, production content, a scientific diagnostic-accuracy
study, or pesticide/product advice. Field validation tests architecture,
usability, continuity, and governance. It is not scientific proof of diagnostic
accuracy unless a separate authorized study is designed.

## Fastest Safe Path

Speed comes from validating one value-bearing slice at a time, preparing the
Rice Knowledge Vertical Slice and Human Review operation early, running only
dependency-safe work in parallel, and stopping scope that is not necessary for
the next pilot. Manual work is retained where automation would add governance risk.

## Owner Decisions

Explicit Owner approval is required for increment scope; pilot crop and
investigation family; participants; reviewer assignment; sensitive-data
handling; case sample; field-validation start; stop-condition resolution;
increment acceptance; expansion; and production eligibility. Approval is
recorded with basis and does not substitute for scientific or governance review.
