# Field Validation Plan

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Validation Boundary

The pilot evaluates user value, workflow completeness, field practicality,
evidence quality/context, gap visibility, question usefulness, reviewer
usability, explanation completeness, follow-up continuity, and governance
compliance. It is not scientific proof of diagnostic accuracy.

## Validation Rounds

| Round | Purpose, participants, prerequisites, and cases | Activities and observations | Success/failure, stop, revision, and exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Internal walkthrough | validate logical journey; SPA, Product, Agronomist, Knowledge, Governance reviewers; approved documents; synthetic or previously documented cases only | walk stages, closures, unresolved handling, handoff, roles, terminology, provenance; collect missing/duplicated responsibility and comprehension | success: coherent complete journey; failure: gaps/overlap; stop on boundary contradiction; revise documents/content; exit by gate approval; not field validation |
| 1 — SPA Investigation Capture | test A in real remote/field work; trained SPAs; approved sample and handling; incomplete simple/difficult cases | receive, report, observe, identify gaps, ask, capture evidence, save/resume, prepare package; observe time, burden, missingness, question utility, friction, terminology, connectivity, duplication, provenance | success: reviewable package without forced certainty; failure: unusable burden/lost context; stop on safety/privacy/data loss/layer collapse; revise A; exit when Gate F evidence accepted |
| 2 — SPA plus Human Review | test B; SPAs and qualified reviewers; validated A packages, Vertical Slice, role matrix | request/accept/return, compare candidates, handle conflict/disagreement, record reviewed/unresolved finding; collect readiness, sufficiency judgment, clarification, turnaround, clarity, honesty, SPA understanding, traceability, earlier-detectable gaps | success: explainable bounded review; failure: reconstruction outside case/hidden rank/forced result; stop on unavailable authority or status confusion; revise A/B; exit after accepted review operation |
| 3 — Follow-up and Outcome continuity | test C; SPA, participants, reviewers; reviewed/unresolved outputs, ownership, approved terminology | present output, record Decision/Action, schedule/follow up, capture new Observation/Outcome/unexpected event, reopen, nominate learning; observe separation, continuity, integrity, ownership | success: history-preserving continuation; failure: causal inference/rewrite/abandoned follow-up; stop on promotion or provenance loss; revise C; exit after continuity evidence accepted |
| 4 — Integrated MVP pilot | test one complete flow; approved SPAs/reviewers and diverse consented cases; A–C validated and all gates ready | Case -> Capture -> Review -> reviewed/unresolved Finding -> Decision -> Action -> Follow-up -> Outcome -> Learning Candidate; collect end-to-end and governance evidence | success: core continuity without external reasoning tool or deferred AI; failure: systemic boundary/burden/review breakdown; mandatory stops apply; revise affected increment; exit by Owner pilot acceptance |

## Structured Debrief

After every round, record what the SPA attempted; what happened; where work
stopped; missing information; helpful/confusing questions; difficult evidence;
reviewer needs not received; work performed outside the product; what to remove,
simplify, or keep manual; governance changes; and possible Learning Candidates.
Debrief findings do not automatically become Product or Knowledge changes.

## Observation Responsibilities

Validation evidence distinguishes user report, direct observation of product
use, conceptual workflow log, reviewer judgment, audit evidence, field debrief,
and governance finding. Each item retains author, method, case scope, time,
limitations, and review status.
