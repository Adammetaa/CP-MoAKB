# Pilot Case Selection and Sampling

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Selection Principles

Pilot cases reflect real approved SPA workload and include incomplete
information, remote and field channels, simple and difficult investigations,
unresolved cases, conflicts, additional-evidence needs, and practical follow-up.
They remain within the approved crop and investigation family. Selection must
not favor only easy success or exclude honest unresolved outcomes.

## Exclusion or Separate Governance

Emergency human-safety cases, suspected illegal pesticide use, active regulatory
disputes, work outside reviewer competence, personally sensitive cases without
consent, cases requiring unavailable laboratory confirmation, and cases with no
practical follow-up opportunity are excluded or handled under separately
approved safeguards. Exclusion reasons and resulting sample limitations remain visible.

## Staged Planning Bands

| Round | Future planning range | Sampling purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Internal walkthrough | 3–5 cases | expose logical gaps using synthetic or documented cases |
| Capture validation | 5–10 cases | vary channel, completeness, burden, and evidence capture |
| Human Review validation | 5–10 cases | vary difficulty, conflict, evidence sufficiency, and reviewer return |
| Follow-up validation | 5–10 cases | vary outcome availability, follow-up timing, and reopening |
| Integrated pilot | 10–20 cases | demonstrate diverse end-to-end continuity |

These are planning ranges, not statistical requirements or final targets. Final
counts depend on user and reviewer capacity, diversity, field season, approved
scope, recurrence or saturation of friction, follow-up opportunity, privacy, and
Governance approval.

## Sample Account

For each round, the validation owner explains the intended diversity, inclusion
and exclusion decisions, recruitment channel, consent/sensitivity handling,
reviewer fit, known bias, attrition, unavailable follow-up, and why additional
cases would or would not add learning. Completion counts alone do not establish success.

## Owner Approvals

Owner approval is explicit for pilot crop/family, participants, reviewer
assignment, sensitive-data handling, sample, field start, material sample
changes, and any expansion. Scientific, privacy, and governance approvals remain
separate where required.
