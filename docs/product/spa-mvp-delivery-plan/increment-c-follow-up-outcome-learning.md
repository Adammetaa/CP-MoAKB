# Increment C — Follow-up, Outcome, and Learning Candidate

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Objective and Value

Enable an SPA to preserve what happened after review and create a governed
feedback loop without rewriting the original investigation. The case can show
what was presented, decided, done, and later observed.

## Included Capabilities

- reviewed output presentation with uncertainty and unresolved issues;
- separate Decision and Action recording;
- follow-up scheduling, ownership, and later Observation capture;
- attributable treatment or management event recording where applicable;
- bounded Outcome, unresolved Outcome, and adverse/unexpected event capture;
- Case closure or reopening with history preserved;
- Learning Candidate, knowledge-gap, and workflow-friction nomination; and
- Outcome and nomination provenance.

## Excluded Capabilities

Automatic causal attribution, efficacy claims, Learning-to-Knowledge promotion,
self-learning, enterprise analytics, and recommendation optimization are excluded.

## Dependencies and Readiness

Increment B must consistently produce reviewed or explicitly unresolved
findings. Decision and Action boundaries, Outcome terminology, follow-up
ownership, closure/reopening rules, Learning Candidate governance, privacy, and
no-promotion rules must be approved. Required review capacity includes SPA
follow-up ownership and Knowledge Review for nominations.

## Validation and Success

Evidence includes observed separation of Decision, Action, and Outcome;
follow-up completion/deferral; new Observation provenance; unexpected-event
capture; reopening; historical integrity audit; and Learning Candidate pending
status. Success means an SPA resumes from the prior case without external tools
and records bounded outcomes without causality claims.

Failure includes Decision inferred as Action, Outcome overwriting history,
efficacy language without authority, abandoned follow-up, or learning bypassing
review. Stop for historical rewrite, automatic causal claims, lost provenance,
privacy breach, or automatic knowledge promotion.

## Rollback, Risks, and Expansion

Rollback pauses follow-up functions, returns to manual external follow-up,
reduces event scope, withdraws learning nomination capability, reopens affected
cases, and notifies users without deleting history. Risks include weak ownership,
loss to external tools, efficacy inference, incomplete field season, and learning
pressure. Pilot completion requires reliable continuity, governed nominations,
resolved critical issues, and Owner acceptance.
