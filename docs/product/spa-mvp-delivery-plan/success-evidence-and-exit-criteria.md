# Success Evidence and Exit Criteria

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Observable Success Evidence

- SPA completes a structured Case under real remote or field conditions.
- Report and Direct Observation remain distinct.
- missing information and uncertainty stay visible without forced certainty.
- attached material retains source/context and quality/relevance distinctions.
- reviewer understands the Case without externally reconstructing core reasoning.
- clarification requests, returns, and ownership are traceable.
- reviewed Finding has identifiable reviewer and complete bounded rationale.
- unresolved Cases close honestly.
- follow-up resumes from the prior Case and Outcome does not overwrite records.
- Learning Candidate remains review-pending and no automatic Diagnosis occurs.
- users do not require external chat or notebook for core Case continuity.

Evidence types remain distinct: user report, direct observation, workflow log,
reviewer judgment, audit evidence, field debrief, and governance finding.

## Failure Evidence

Failure signals include inability to complete cases during work; duplicate entry;
report/Observation merging; false negatives from missing data; lost evidence
source/context; diagnosis-leading questions; repeated external reconstruction;
unclear review roles; ownerless stalls; forced findings; premature Management
Options; historical rewrite; learning bypass; external-chat dependence; offline
data loss; or dependence on deferred AI.

## Measurement Candidates

Future measures may include time to create a Case and reach review readiness;
clarification return percentage; missing evidence and unanswered question counts;
review turnaround; explanation/provenance completeness; follow-up and Outcome
completion; unresolved rate; external-tool dependency; reported SPA burden;
reviewer usability; and unsupported-recommendation reduction. No final target is
set, and speed must not be optimized alone.

## Increment Exit Criteria

- **A exits** when diverse SPAs repeatedly produce traceable review packages at
  acceptable burden, with incomplete recovery and no critical boundary failure.
- **B exits** when qualified reviewers can return, disagree, escalate, and record
  reviewed or unresolved outcomes with complete provenance and no hidden rank.
- **C exits** when Decision, Action, follow-up, Outcome, reopening, and Learning
  Candidate nomination preserve separation and history.
- **Pilot exits** when all delivery gates pass, integrated cases demonstrate core
  continuity, critical issues are resolved, residual risk is Owner-accepted, and
  no mandatory stop remains open.

## Expansion Evidence

Expansion requires both positive value evidence and absence or resolution of
critical failure evidence. A round may succeed with unresolved cases; it cannot
succeed by hiding unresolved work or excluding difficult cases.
