# Human Review and Authority Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Role Principle

Roles express case-specific authority and expertise, not organization-specific
job titles, permanent identities, access-control implementation, or hierarchy.
Every review records purpose, scope, assigned human, conflicts, rationale, and limits.

## Authority Matrix

| Role | View and author | Edit and challenge | Approve, reject, return, unresolved, withdraw, escalate |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPA | view coordinated case within purpose; author intake, direct Observations, question uses, evidence collection context, provisional candidates, coordination records, follow-up | edit own drafts with history; challenge reports, evidence roles, candidates, and case completeness within authority | approve routine SPA coordination for purpose; reject/return inadequate intake; mark unresolved; withdraw own nomination; escalate science, safety, regulation, and authority |
| Agronomist | view scientific case package; author qualified observation, differential comments, finding review, escalation response, and eligibility review | edit own review with history; challenge candidates, criteria, evidence interpretation, and SPA finding drafts | approve/reject bounded agronomic review; return for evidence; mark unresolved; withdraw own draft; escalate scientific, regulatory, or governance issues |
| Scientific Reviewer | view relevant scientific material; author specialist interpretation, source-conflict, unusual-pattern, and knowledge-gap review | challenge methods, identity, laboratory or source interpretation | approve/reject scientific review for scope; return/mark unresolved; withdraw draft; escalate beyond expertise or authority |
| Knowledge Reviewer | view governed references and nominations; author applicability, terminology, source authority, and knowledge-change review | challenge hidden, outdated, unsupported, or misapplied knowledge | approve/reject knowledge conformance or nomination stage; return/mark unresolved; escalate scientific/governance needs |
| Governance Reviewer | view necessary governance/audit material; author publication, regulatory-boundary, sensitive-information, policy-exception, and audit-dispute findings | challenge authority, process, visibility, retention, and eligibility | approve/reject only within governance authority; return/mark unresolved/withdraw governance disposition; escalate missing scientific or legal authority |

No role may approve outside assigned authority or substitute for a missing
specialist. Review approval does not promote Observation to Evidence, candidate
to Diagnosis, finding to Claim, option to Recommendation, or eligibility to publication.

## Mandatory Review Points

Agronomist or qualified expert review is mandatory before the MVP records a
reviewed Investigation Finding. Additional Scientific, Knowledge, or Governance
review is required for unusual or disputed science, source conflict, knowledge
change, safety, regulation, sensitive information, publication eligibility,
policy exceptions, or audit disputes.

## Return and Escalation

A reviewer may request clarification, identify a precise Evidence Need, preserve
conflict, mark the matter unresolved, reject for stated purpose, or escalate.
Silence, elapsed time, status, majority, or completion is not approval. Review
bottlenecks must result in visible pending status and safe pause—not automatic acceptance.

## Authority Safety

Requests for pesticide or product recommendation, suspected resistance,
human/crop safety risk, regulatory questions, unknown identity, repeated failure,
or work beyond SPA competence are routed to separately qualified authority. The
MVP does not provide the requested substantive advice itself.
