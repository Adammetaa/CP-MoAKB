# SPA End-to-End Journey

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Interpretation

The journey defines human objectives and traceable handoffs, not executable
workflow logic. “Minimum” is purpose-specific; a human may pause, revisit,
skip with reason, close unresolved, or escalate.

## Stages 1–8

| Stage | SPA objective and minimum information | Permitted / prohibited | Output, blocks, review, status, and field use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Login | establish attributable working responsibility; minimum authorized identity context | access assigned work; do not infer authority from access | accountable session context; block unclear authority; no case status change; minimize field interruption |
| Receive Job or Case | acknowledge source, subject, urgency, and safety signals | accept, redirect, reject, or request context; do not diagnose | receipt record; human intake review; Received or Rejected; phone/chat compatible |
| Review Initial Report | preserve reporter wording, source, time, and uncertainty | classify as reported; do not rewrite as Observation | initial report and gaps; block unsafe or unattributable handling; Received |
| Prepare Investigation | plan channel, contact, field safety, evidence needs, and required expertise | prepare questions and capture plan; do not presuppose cause | preparation notes; SPA review; Intake in progress; supports remote or field visit |
| Open or Create Case | establish bounded subject, responsible SPA, reporter, concern, and safety basis | create or link case manually; do not merge duplicates silently | case container; block insufficient opening basis; Draft or Intake in progress |
| Record Reported Information | retain exact meaning, attribution, language, and context | record reports and contradictions; do not mark verified | reported account; SPA review; Intake in progress; rapid capture allowed |
| Record Direct Observations | capture observer, method, time, place, result, and limitations | author bounded Observation; do not infer Evidence or Diagnosis | Observation record; provenance review; Observation review; offline capture permitted |
| Review Information States | distinguish known, reported, observed, measured, missing, conflicting, and review states | assess explicitly; do not map blanks to “no” | state account; SPA review; incomplete or ready; low cognitive load required |

## Stages 9–16

| Stage | SPA objective and minimum information | Permitted / prohibited | Output, blocks, review, status, and field use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identify Information Gaps | state unresolved requirements and why they matter | open, refine, or defer gaps; do not create negative Observations | gap list; block safety-critical gaps; Information incomplete; visible in field |
| Select and Ask Questions | manually choose pattern, author instance, and preserve scope | ask, adapt, skip with reason; no automatic selection or decision tree | Question Instance and response expectation; SPA neutrality review; active/incomplete |
| Collect Evidence | request or capture material for an explicit need | attach with provenance and consent; do not accept as Evidence automatically | inventory and custody context; block unsafe collection; Awaiting evidence; capture before spraying where possible |
| Review Evidence Quality and Relevance | separate usability from bearing on a precise issue | assess quality and relevance independently; no sufficiency automation | limitations and scoped evidence roles; qualified review; investigation status |
| Consult Knowledge References | identify exact governed sources and applicability | mark consulted, applicable, challenged, outdated, or pending; no hidden knowledge | consultation record; Knowledge review as needed; investigation status; cached availability disclosed |
| Author Hypothesis Candidates | state provisional alternatives with rationale and limits | explicitly author multiple where appropriate; do not diagnose or rank | candidate set; SPA authorship and Agronomist review pending; Investigation in progress |
| Perform Differential Comparison | apply explained criteria to competing candidates | record support, challenge, missing, conflict, irrelevance; no score or winner | provisional comparison; block inadequate criteria; Differential review |
| Request Human Review | submit traceable package, review question, uncertainty, and escalation basis | assign qualified reviewer; do not imply approval | review request; required expert; Awaiting Agronomist review; remote handoff supported |

## Stages 17–25

| Stage | SPA objective and minimum information | Permitted / prohibited | Output, blocks, review, status, and field use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receive Reviewed Finding | understand bounded finding, rationale, limits, dissent, and unresolved issues | accept for stated use, return, or escalate; do not promote to Claim or Diagnosis | reviewed finding; mandatory reviewer; Reviewed or returned |
| Review Management Options Eligibility | verify readiness, review, uncertainty, safety, and regulatory prerequisites | record eligibility only; no pesticide or product recommendation | eligibility explanation; authorized review; eligible or blocked |
| Present Reviewed Output | communicate source distinctions, findings, limits, and eligible content | present clearly; do not hide provisional or unresolved status | attributable explanation; SPA review; status unchanged; usable by phone or field |
| Record Decision | preserve user's explicit choice, basis, timing, and deferral | record chosen, declined, or deferred; do not infer Action | Decision record; decision-maker authority; Decision recorded |
| Record Action | capture what actually occurred, actor, time, place, and deviations | record attributable act; do not infer Outcome | Action record; provenance review; Action recorded |
| Schedule Follow-up | state purpose, owner, timing basis, evidence needs, and safety | schedule or defer with reason; do not predict outcome | follow-up plan; SPA ownership; Follow-up scheduled; offline reminder expectation only |
| Record Outcome | capture later report, Observation, or measurement with context | record bounded outcome; do not rewrite history or infer efficacy | Outcome record; review as needed; Outcome recorded |
| Capture Learning Candidate | nominate a reviewable gap or pattern with source case linkage | nominate only; do not create Knowledge automatically | provisional Learning Candidate; Knowledge review pending; case status unchanged |
| Close or Reopen Case | record closure category or new reopening basis | close resolved or unresolved, or reopen explicitly; do not imply Diagnosis | closure/reopening rationale; authorized review; Closed or Reopened |

## No Skipped Reasoning

Presentation may condense stages, but evidence, knowledge, candidate, comparison,
and review boundaries cannot be omitted. Remote and field channels use the same
semantics and preserve channel-specific limitations.
