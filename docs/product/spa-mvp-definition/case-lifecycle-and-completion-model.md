# Case Lifecycle and Completion Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Lifecycle Interpretation

Stages describe governed case standing, not automatic state transitions. Entry,
exit, and ownership are explicit human decisions. Every stage preserves visible
unresolved conditions and audit history.

## Lifecycle Stages

| Stage | Meaning and entry criteria | Permitted / prohibited, owner, and exits | Unresolved conditions, outputs, and audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft | bounded case preparation before formal receipt; authored basis exists | SPA edits intake; no reviewed claims; exit Received, Withdrawn, Rejected | gaps visible; draft summary; audit creator and changes |
| Received | attributable case accepted for triage | SPA records source/safety; no diagnosis; exit Intake or Rejected | opening gaps; receipt output; audit channel/time |
| Intake in progress | case bounds and context being established | SPA clarifies; no readiness claim; exit Incomplete or Ready | missing basis; intake summary; audit sources |
| Information incomplete | required information for a purpose is unresolved | SPA asks/records states; no forced certainty; exit Intake, Awaiting evidence, Ready, Withdrawn | explicit gaps; unanswered questions; audit attempts |
| Ready for investigation | human confirms minimum bounded readiness | SPA begins inquiry; no implication of completeness; exit Investigation or Incomplete | nonblocking gaps remain; readiness rationale audited |
| Investigation in progress | observations, gaps, evidence, knowledge, and candidates organized | SPA authors provisional content; no Diagnosis/ranking; exits Awaiting evidence/SPA review/Differential | provisional outputs; audit bases |
| Awaiting evidence | explicit needs remain pending | SPA requests/attaches material; no absence inference; exit Investigation/Incomplete/Withdrawn | needs and limitations; audit custody/attempts |
| Awaiting SPA review | case material awaits accountable SPA check | SPA returns, accepts for coordination, or escalates; no expert substitution; exits Investigation/Agronomist review | review question; audit disposition |
| Awaiting Agronomist review | qualified review is assigned | Agronomist challenges/returns/reviews; no silent approval; exits Investigation/Differential/Reviewed | uncertainty and conflicts; review audit |
| Differential review | candidates compared by explained criteria | SPA/Agronomist assess roles; no scores/winner; exits Investigation/Agronomist review/Reviewed | comparison and adverse evidence; audit criteria |
| Reviewed | mandatory review completed for bounded finding | authorized reviewer records finding/returns; no Diagnosis promotion; exits eligible/Decision pending/Follow-up/Closed | findings and unresolved issues; audit authority |
| Management-options eligible | prerequisites and future authority reviewed | consider eligible governed options only; no automatic Recommendation; exits Decision pending/Reviewed/Closed | eligibility basis; audit safety/regulatory review |
| Decision pending | reviewed output communicated, user choice outstanding | SPA clarifies options/status; no decision inference; exits Decision recorded/Follow-up/Closed | pending reason; communication audit |
| Decision recorded | attributable choice captured | record chosen/declined/deferred; no Action inference; exits Action/Follow-up/Closed | Decision output; actor/basis audit |
| Action recorded | attributable actual act captured | record context/deviation; no Outcome inference; exits Follow-up/Outcome pending/Closed | Action output; provenance audit |
| Follow-up scheduled | bounded later review is planned | SPA updates attempts; no predicted Outcome; exits Outcome pending/Investigation/Closed | plan and owner; audit timing changes |
| Outcome pending | follow-up due or evidence awaited | capture later report/Observation; no efficacy inference; exits Outcome recorded/Follow-up/Closed | outstanding needs; audit attempts |
| Outcome recorded | bounded later result captured | review and contextualize; no historical rewrite; exits Closed/Reopened/Follow-up | Outcome and limits; source/method audit |
| Closed | authorized work ended with closure category | preserve summary; no confirmed Diagnosis implication; exits Reopened only | unresolved matters retained; closure audit |
| Reopened | new basis justifies resumed work | revisit prior assumptions; no history erasure; exits appropriate active stage | reopening rationale; initiator/basis audit |
| Withdrawn | participation or inquiry ended without adjudication | preserve required duties; no resolution inference; exits Reopened under authority | withdrawal limits; authority audit |
| Rejected | matter invalid or unacceptable for stated purpose | state failed criteria; no deletion/diagnosis; exits Draft/Received only on new basis | rejection reason; reviewer/appeal audit |

## Completion Definitions

| Completion | Required conceptual result |
|---|---|
| Intake complete | case bounds, source, responsibility, safety screen, reported concern, and material missing states reviewed |
| Investigation ready | sufficient observation/context basis, gaps, responsible SPA, and no critical unresolved block for the bounded inquiry |
| Evidence ready | relevant needs, material inventory, provenance, quality, limitations, and scoped relevance review available; not necessarily sufficient |
| Differential ready | explicit candidates, rationale, criteria, evidence roles, adverse/missing material, and unresolved issues visible |
| Human-review ready | exact review question, complete traceable package for purpose, authority need, conflicts, and uncertainty disclosed |
| Reviewed finding complete | authorized reviewer, bounded finding, rationale, evidence and knowledge basis, limitations, dissent, and unresolved issues recorded |
| Decision complete | attributable decision-maker, choice/deferral, basis, timing, and reviewed information communicated |
| Action complete | actual actor, act, time, place, source, deviations, and safety context recorded |
| Follow-up complete | planned follow-up performed or explicitly closed/deferred with reason and remaining needs |
| Outcome complete | bounded result, source/observer, time, context, uncertainty, and review need recorded |
| Case closed | closure category, authority, explanation, outstanding risk, retention, follow-up, and audit reviewed |

## Closure Categories

A case may close as resolved, unresolved with explanation, withdrawn,
insufficient evidence, referred externally, duplicate, invalid, or outside
scope. “Resolved” means the bounded investigation purpose was addressed; no
closure category implies confirmed Diagnosis.
