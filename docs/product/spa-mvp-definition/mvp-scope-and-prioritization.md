# MVP Scope and Prioritization

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Must Have

The minimum usable release includes exactly the capabilities needed for one
complete manual case:

1. Case Intake;
2. Reported Information Capture;
3. Manual Observation Recording;
4. Information State Review;
5. Information Gap Tracking;
6. Manual Question Selection;
7. Question Response Capture;
8. Evidence Attachment and Inventory;
9. Evidence Need Tracking;
10. Knowledge Reference Access;
11. Manual Hypothesis Candidate Authoring;
12. Manual Differential Comparison;
13. Agronomist or Expert Review;
14. Reviewed Investigation Finding;
15. Explainability View as a conceptual responsibility;
16. Decision Recording;
17. Action Recording;
18. Follow-up Scheduling;
19. Outcome Recording; and
20. Audit and Provenance Visibility.

“View” names a product capability, not a UI layout. Each Must Have contributes
directly to the acceptance case; none contains automation.

## Should Have

- reusable Case Templates;
- field checklist;
- case handoff;
- contradiction alerts that reveal, not resolve, conflict;
- review-return workflow;
- report generation;
- knowledge-gap nomination;
- Learning Candidate capture;
- offline continuity; and
- role-specific visibility.

Offline continuity is strategically important and conceptually defined for the
MVP, but a full synchronization implementation is oversized until technical
authority and safety behavior are separately approved.

## Could Have

- voice-note attachment;
- basic case comparison without causal inference;
- reusable evidence request sets;
- Service Provider read-only access;
- location-aware case grouping; and
- structured summary export.

## Won't Have in First MVP

- automatic diagnosis, question selection, hypothesis ranking, recommendation,
  product selection, Observation extraction, or evidence sufficiency decisions;
- image classification, predictive outbreak or resistance modeling;
- autonomous pesticide selection, field decisions, or farmer advice;
- self-learning from unreviewed cases;
- product sales optimization, enterprise analytics, automated regulatory decisions;
- multi-crop production content or production Product Knowledge.

## Oversized Items Removed from Must Have

Reusable templates, generalized report generation, full offline synchronization,
multi-role portals, learning analytics, production knowledge population, and all
AI assistance are outside the first complete-case Critical Path. Their boundaries
may be prepared, but implementation and acceptance are deferred.

## Prioritization Rule

Safety, epistemic separation, manual completion, traceability, review, and field
continuity outrank convenience or automation. A feature that cannot preserve
these boundaries is deferred rather than partially automated.
