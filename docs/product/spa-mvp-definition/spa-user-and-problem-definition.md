# SPA User and Problem Definition

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Primary User

The SPA coordinates real remote and field investigation under time, evidence,
connectivity, authority, and safety constraints. The SPA may receive calls,
chats, images, or field requests; contact a Service Provider or farmer; prepare
and travel; observe directly; inspect distribution and crop stage; review water,
weather, management, and prior applications; request evidence; consult knowledge;
compare candidates; escalate; explain reviewed output; and follow up.

## Secondary Review Participants

- Agronomists review scientific interpretation, differential comparison,
  findings, escalation, and future Management Options eligibility.
- Scientific Reviewers address disputed interpretation, unusual patterns,
  source conflict, and scientific knowledge gaps.
- Knowledge Reviewers assess applicability, terminology, source authority, and
  knowledge-change nominations.
- Governance Reviewers assess publication, regulation, sensitive information,
  policy exceptions, and audit disputes.

These responsibilities are contextual roles, not permanent job or technical identities.

## Future Users

Service Providers, Dealer Advisors, Crop Advisors, enterprise users, and farmers
may receive later bounded experiences. Their future needs must not dilute SPA
case coordination or introduce autonomous advice into the first MVP.

## User Problems

The SPA must work when identity, growth stage, location, onset, management
history, evidence, or terminology is missing or conflicting. The product must
reduce cognitive burden while preventing shortcuts: report must not become
Observation, image must not become Evidence automatically, local name must not
become scientific identity, and candidate must not become Diagnosis.

## Jobs to Be Done

- build a trustworthy account from fragmented sources;
- see what is known, unknown, conflicting, or blocked;
- decide manually which neutral question to ask next;
- capture contextual material before interpretation;
- prepare a reviewable case package for an Agronomist;
- explain how evidence and knowledge relate to competing candidates;
- communicate reviewed findings without hiding uncertainty; and
- preserve what was decided, done, and later observed.

## Anti-chatbot Controls

The MVP does not answer outside a case, hide source material, infer intent from
conversation, improvise knowledge, select a diagnostic path, or optimize for a
fast answer. Structured roles, manual selection, governed references, review
states, and audit history prevent it from becoming another agricultural chatbot.
