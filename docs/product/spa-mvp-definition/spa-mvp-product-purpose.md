# SPA MVP Product Purpose

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Exact Problem

SPAs receive incomplete, inconsistent, and often interpretive crop-protection
information from farmers, Service Providers, field visits, images, prior spray
records, Agronomists, dealers, and local terminology. Sources, observation
bases, uncertainty, evidence relevance, and review authority are easily blurred.
The result can be premature certainty, weak follow-up, and unsupported advice.

## MVP Outcome

The MVP helps an SPA structure a bounded case; preserve what was reported;
record direct Observations separately; identify known, missing, conflicting,
unknown, unavailable, and not-applicable information; manually ask an
appropriate question; request relevant evidence; consult governed knowledge;
author and compare provisional explanations; request expert review; explain a
reviewed finding or future Management Options eligibility; and record Decision,
Action, follow-up, and Outcome separately.

It does not promise a confirmed Diagnosis from incomplete evidence. An honest,
traceable unresolved case is a valid product outcome.

## Product Vision Boundary

The product coordinates investigation rather than answering free-form
agricultural questions. Every substantive case statement has a declared role,
source, context, and review status. Conversation may be an intake channel, but
unstructured dialogue cannot bypass case structure, Question Bank governance,
evidence review, or human authority.

## Useful Without Automation

Before Image Intelligence, the MVP reduces ambiguity by preserving source
separation, guiding manual evidence capture, revealing gaps, organizing expert
handoffs, and generating explainable case summaries. Its core value is higher
quality human investigation, not model accuracy.

## Product Knowledge Boundary

Future Product Knowledge may describe product identity, active ingredient,
formulation, approved crop use, target organism, label constraints, registration,
Mode of Action, resistance considerations, safety constraints, and application
limitations. This sprint authors none of that content or logic. Product Knowledge
must never bypass investigation readiness, evidence review, scientific review,
regulatory review, or human authority; it must not prioritize company products
or optimize sales.

## Definition of Success

The MVP succeeds when one complete manual case can remain incomplete where
honest, obtain bounded review, explain every major output, preserve field
continuity, and avoid unsupported recommendations.
