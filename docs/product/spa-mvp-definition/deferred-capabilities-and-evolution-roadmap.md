# Deferred Capabilities and Evolution Roadmap

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Deferral Rule

Deferral is a safety and scope decision, not roadmap approval. Every capability
requires separate evidence, authority, product validation, architecture review,
and implementation sprint before use.

## Deferred Capabilities

| Capability | Why deferred | Prerequisite | Risk if early | Future dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Image Intelligence | MVP value does not depend on classification | governed image corpus, labels, consent, evaluation, human-review policy | image becomes Diagnosis and bias is hidden | Observation Workspace and reviewable suggestion boundary |
| automatic Observation extraction | capture meaning and context remain human-authored | neutral vocabulary, provenance, uncertainty, correction workflow | reports/media become false Observations | Observation Architecture and human affirmation |
| automatic question selection | Question Bank permits manual consideration only | validated applicability assistance and bias review | decision tree and diagnosis-leading path | Gap and Question Workspaces |
| automatic evidence sufficiency decisions | sufficiency is purpose- and reviewer-dependent | evidence standards, calibrated assistance, override/audit | missing evidence is concealed or accepted | Evidence and Review Workspaces |
| automatic hypothesis ranking | hidden rank violates differential boundary | separately governed advisory research and explanations | winner becomes Diagnosis | Hypothesis/Differential and human-review controls |
| automatic diagnosis | outside platform and MVP authority | diagnostic governance, qualified evidence, safety validation | unsafe false certainty | full reviewed investigation chain |
| automatic recommendation | Recommendation governance is absent | diagnostic, regulatory, safety, product, and authority frameworks | unsafe or unlawful advice | reviewed findings and options boundary |
| automatic product selection | conflicts with neutrality and product boundary | governed Product Knowledge and non-commercial review | sales optimization overrides science | Product Knowledge and regulatory review |
| resistance prediction | requires specialist data and validation | governed longitudinal evidence and scientific review | unsupported resistance claim | Scientific Review and future analytics |
| outbreak prediction | requires population and environmental models | validated multi-source data and uncertainty governance | harmful forecast confidence | enterprise analytics and human review |
| autonomous field decisions | human Decision remains protected | explicit authority, safety, liability, and override governance | unsafe action without consent | Decision/Action boundary |
| farmer-facing autonomous advice | farmer is not primary MVP user | bounded user research, language, safety, review, escalation | unreviewed advice at scale | future advisor experience |
| self-learning from raw cases | cases are not approved Knowledge | Learning Candidate and Knowledge Review pipeline | error and bias become institutional knowledge | Learning Workspace and package governance |
| real-time enterprise analytics | first MVP proves one manual case | multi-case governance, privacy, metrics, permissions | surveillance, weak aggregation, scope explosion | enterprise oversight architecture |
| multi-crop production rollout | no production content exists | governed crop packages, localization, evidence, qualified review | shallow or unsafe generalization | package architecture and Crop Advisor evolution |

## Strategic Evolution

> SPA -> Crop Advisor -> Knowledge-driven Advisor -> Explainable AI Advisor ->
> Enterprise Crop Intelligence Platform

Evolution adds Service Provider and Dealer Advisor access, an Agronomist
workbench, multi-crop packages, governed image intelligence, Observation and
question suggestions, evidence sufficiency assistance, hypothesis suggestions,
Management Option support, enterprise oversight, and learning analytics. Future
AI remains advisory, explainable, reversible, and subject to human review. The
manual provenance chain remains the stable foundation.

## Architecture Risks

| Risk | Cause and consequence | Mitigation principle | Deferral or review requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| agricultural chatbot drift | free-form answering bypasses case roles and sources | require bounded case, structured artifacts, and explanation chain | reject autonomous answer features; Product review |
| Question Bank as decision tree | triggers or order become executable branches | manual selection and explicit skip reason | defer selection automation; neutrality review |
| Observation becomes Diagnosis | labels or images encode interpretation | enforce layer separation and provenance | Observation/Agronomist review |
| quality confused with relevance | usable material assumed probative | record independent assessments per purpose | Evidence review required |
| Product Knowledge overrides science | commercial content drives options | separate authority, disclose use, prohibit prioritization | Scientific, regulatory, and Governance review |
| SPA exceeds authority | urgency or convenience bypasses escalation | visible authority limits and safe pause | mandatory expert escalation |
| incomplete treated as confirmed | completion pressure hides gaps | honest closure and explicit missing states | reviewed-finding gate |
| review bottleneck | scarce experts delay cases | visible queues, bounded packages, unresolved closure | no timeout approval; capacity review |
| excessive field entry | oversized intake disrupts observation and safety | purpose-minimum data and incomplete save | field usability review |
| module duplication | workspaces own the same semantics | single responsibility and reference-based coordination | architecture review before extension |
| workflow fragmentation | channel or module loses context | shared case provenance and bounded handoffs | journey review |
| hidden ranking | order, color, or completeness implies winner | no scores; disclose ordering neutrality | differential review |
| premature automation | convenience outruns governance | manual-first acceptance case | defer all listed automation |
| weak follow-up | investigation stops at reviewed output | Must Have follow-up and Outcome responsibilities | closure review |
| Learning Candidate bypass | nomination treated as Knowledge | mandatory Knowledge Review/package process | no automatic promotion |
| privacy/location sensitivity | detailed field/person data overexposed | purpose-bound visibility and sensitivity classification | privacy/Governance review |
| offline inconsistency | local and reviewed versions diverge | visible unsynchronized status and later conflict review | technical design deferred |
| excessive MVP scope | enterprise and AI features dilute complete case | prioritize one manual Critical Path | scope gate each sprint |

## Evolution Constraint

No future capability may silently weaken existing case, evidence, knowledge,
review, visibility, or lifecycle boundaries. Evolution extends reviewed modules;
it does not replace the human-governed chain with opaque prediction.
