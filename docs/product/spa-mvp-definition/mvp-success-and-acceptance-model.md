# MVP Success and Acceptance Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Success Dimensions

Success is evaluated through product and investigation quality, not model
accuracy alone:

- case completeness for its stated purpose, with honest incomplete states;
- visibility and resolution history of Information Gaps;
- evidence provenance, quality limitations, and relevance traceability;
- review turnaround and visible pending or returned work;
- explanation completeness across every reviewed output;
- SPA usability and low-burden field practicality;
- honesty of unresolved cases and absence of manufactured certainty;
- follow-up completion or explicit deferral;
- attributable Outcome capture without causal inference;
- knowledge-gap and Learning Candidate capture;
- reduction of unsupported recommendations; and
- preservation of privacy, authority, and auditability.

Numeric targets are future measurement candidates only. They require a later
measurement plan, baseline, definitions, privacy review, and anti-gaming review.

## Complete Manual Case Acceptance

The definition is acceptable only if a future MVP can demonstrate that:

1. an SPA opens a bounded case;
2. reported information is recorded with source;
3. direct Observations are recorded separately;
4. missing information remains explicit;
5. the SPA selects Question Patterns manually;
6. responses and uncertainty are recorded;
7. contextual material is attached;
8. evidence limitations remain visible;
9. governed Knowledge References and their use states are recorded;
10. multiple provisional candidates are authored where appropriate;
11. candidates are compared with explained criteria and no hidden ranking;
12. an Agronomist reviews the package;
13. a bounded reviewed finding is recorded;
14. uncertainty and unresolved issues remain visible;
15. Management Options appear only under separately reviewed eligibility;
16. the user's Decision is recorded separately;
17. actual Action is recorded separately;
18. follow-up is scheduled or explicitly deferred;
19. a bounded Outcome is recorded;
20. a Learning Candidate may be nominated without becoming Knowledge;
21. every major output exposes provenance and review; and
22. no automatic Diagnosis occurs.

## Failure Conditions

Acceptance fails if the product answers as an unstructured chatbot, skips
evidence or review, hides knowledge use, treats an image as diagnosis, forces
missing values, ranks candidates implicitly, presents an option as
Recommendation, infers Action or Outcome, or allows learning to bypass governance.

## Reporting Acceptance

Minimum reporting responsibilities include case, Observation, missing
information, evidence inventory and limitations, candidate comparison, reviewer
finding, unresolved issues, eligibility, Decision/Action, follow-up, and Outcome.
Every output is marked provisional, reviewed, unresolved, rejected, withdrawn,
publication eligible, or internal only as appropriate. Layout is not defined.
