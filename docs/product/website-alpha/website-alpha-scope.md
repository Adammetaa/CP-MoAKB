# Website Alpha Scope

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Alpha Outcome

Website Alpha demonstrates that CP-MoAKB can expose a trustworthy,
knowledge-first and explainable public product surface before SPA case operation
or AI. A visitor can browse eligible knowledge, understand investigation, search
and traverse relationships, inspect traceability, and explore governed documentation.
The Alpha scope remains minimal: every capability not required for this outcome
is classified as later or deferred.

## Included Scope

- governed knowledge browsing;
- explainable Investigation flow;
- search over eligible representations;
- source, provenance, authority, version, review, and limitation traceability;
- asserted knowledge relationships;
- documentation exploration;
- persona-oriented explanation without technical permission implementation;
- Thai-first-compatible and crop-independent content semantics; and
- explicit roadmap and deferred-capability communication.

## Explicit Exclusions

Image Intelligence, Diagnosis, Recommendation, product or pesticide selection,
analytics, automation, enterprise administration, active case work, evidence
upload, Human Review operation, field/offline operation, personalization,
automatic ranking, and all AI are excluded.

## Minimum Acceptance

The architecture is Alpha-ready for later implementation definition when:

- every top-level area has a unique responsibility and navigational relationship;
- every included capability maps to a governed source and visibility boundary;
- Knowledge is referenced rather than duplicated;
- search and relationships cannot imply diagnosis or rank;
- public, future, and deferred states are unmistakable;
- SPA evolution can reuse the architecture without weakening public boundaries; and
- required Product, Architecture, Knowledge, Scientific, Release, and Publication reviews are identified.

## Non-implications

Architecture acceptance does not mean Website implementation, Alpha Release,
public deployment, publication, SPA readiness, Production readiness, or
Enterprise readiness. Those require later governed increments and gates.
