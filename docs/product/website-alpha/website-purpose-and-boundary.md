# Website Purpose and Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Website Alpha provides a coherent public product surface for browsing governed
knowledge and understanding how explainable crop investigation is structured.
It makes authority, provenance, evidence, relationships, uncertainty, review,
version, and limitations discoverable without duplicating or re-authoring knowledge.

## Inclusion Boundary

Alpha may conceptually support governed knowledge browsing, documentation
exploration, search over eligible content, relationship navigation, traceability,
and an explanatory investigation journey. It may describe future SPA and Human
Review experiences while keeping them visibly unavailable in Alpha.

## Exclusion Boundary

Website Alpha is not an agricultural chatbot, diagnosis engine, pesticide
recommendation engine, image classifier, expert replacement, case-management
workspace, field pilot, enterprise administration system, or automated advisor.
It does not accept a symptom and return a conclusion, select questions, assess
evidence, author candidates, rank explanations, or generate Management Options.

## Public-facing Boundary

“Public-facing architecture” describes intended audience orientation, not a
publication decision. Only separately publication-eligible content may later be
shown publicly. Draft, case-bound, sensitive, provisional, internal-review, or
not-publication-eligible material remains excluded.

## Knowledge and Product Separation

The Website presents approved representations of governed objects and documents.
It does not become the source of truth, create Knowledge Objects from page copy,
or change scientific meaning for presentation convenience. Product navigation
cannot override Knowledge, Scientific, Human Review, Design Freeze, or
Publication authority.

## Architecture Boundary

This sprint defines purpose, personas, information architecture, navigation,
capabilities, integration responsibilities, page boundaries, scope, roadmap,
and deferrals. It prescribes no routes, URLs, page layouts, components,
permissions implementation, search engine, indexing, rendering, or hosting.
