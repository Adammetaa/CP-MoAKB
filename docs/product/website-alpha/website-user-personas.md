# Website User Personas

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Persona Interpretation

Personas describe product goals and conceptual visibility, not technical roles,
accounts, permissions, organizational titles, or access-control implementation.
Actual access and publication require separate governance.

| Persona | Goals and responsibilities | Conceptual permissions and knowledge visibility | Future evolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public Visitor | understand the Program, browse eligible knowledge, learn investigation boundaries, verify sources | read only separately publication-eligible representations and public documentation; no cases, drafts, reviewer content, or actions | richer education, localization, and public package exploration |
| Service Provider | understand approved concepts and future service context; communicate without inventing conclusions | public content plus separately authorized service-facing reviewed material; no candidate authoring or review authority in Alpha | bounded read-only SPA outputs and handoff context |
| Service Provider Advisor | prepare for structured investigation and understand governed knowledge/questions | Alpha browsing only; future access to authorized case, Question Pattern, evidence, and explanation responsibilities; provisional material role-bounded | SPA Alpha investigation workspace and field continuity |
| Agronomist | inspect authoritative knowledge and understand review architecture | eligible knowledge and documentation in Alpha; future assigned case evidence, candidates, comparison, and review records | Human Review workbench with scoped scientific authority |
| Knowledge Reviewer | verify terminology, sources, relationships, packages, review and lifecycle meaning | eligible published representations; future internal candidates and exact governance records under assignment | governed authoring/review and knowledge-gap workflows |
| Program Administrator | understand product/program scope, versions, limitations, and governance status | public Alpha content; future internal governance, release, audit, and configuration responsibilities only under explicit authority | enterprise oversight without scientific-authority substitution |

## Persona Boundaries

Visibility does not imply ability to author, approve, release, or publish. One
person may serve several roles, but every act retains its assigned authority.
Program Administrator never becomes a superuser who can bypass Knowledge,
Scientific, Human Review, privacy, Design Freeze, or Publication controls.

## Thai-first and Accessibility Compatibility

All personas may require Thai-first content with explicit English technical
alignment, language provenance, readable uncertainty, and accessible
explanations. Localization must not merge concepts or strengthen claims.
