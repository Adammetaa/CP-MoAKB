# Website Product Vision

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## What Website Alpha Is

Website Alpha is a knowledge-first explanatory product surface. It helps people
discover what CP-MoAKB governs, browse eligible Knowledge Objects and Packages,
understand neutral Observation and Investigation concepts, follow evidence and
review provenance, and reconstruct why an approved representation exists.

It is a foundation for later SPA Alpha, not a reduced SPA workspace. Its Alpha
value is trusted orientation and traceable exploration before operational case work.

## What Website Alpha Is Not

It is not a conversational answer system, agricultural chatbot, disease or
image classifier, Diagnosis engine, pesticide or product recommendation engine,
decision support tool, expert replacement, autonomous advisory system, or sales surface.

## Product Promise

A visitor should be able to answer:

- what CP-MoAKB is and is not;
- which knowledge is available and under what authority;
- how Observation differs from Evidence and Diagnosis;
- how an Investigation exposes gaps, questions, provisional candidates, and review;
- how relationships, sources, versions, limitations, and review decisions connect; and
- which capabilities are Alpha, future, or explicitly prohibited.

## Explainability Principle

Every displayed scientific or governance meaning must link conceptually to its
governed source object, version, provenance, authority, review standing, and
known limitations. Search result order, prominence, or navigation path must not
imply scientific rank, diagnosis likelihood, endorsement, or recommendation.

## Evolution Principle

The architecture supports future role-aware SPA and reviewer experiences by
preserving stable content responsibilities and epistemic boundaries. Future AI
may only be separately governed, advisory, explainable, and Human-reviewed; Alpha
neither includes nor depends on it.
