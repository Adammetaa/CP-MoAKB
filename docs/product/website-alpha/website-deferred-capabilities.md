# Website Deferred Capabilities

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Deferral Principle

Deferred capabilities are not implicitly approved. Each requires a separate
Product Definition, Architecture Review, knowledge and authority readiness,
implementation authorization, validation, release, and—where public—Publication decision.

## SPA Alpha

| Capability | Prerequisites |
|---|---|
| authentication and role-aware workspace | identity/privacy/authority architecture and technical permission review |
| Case Intake and manual Observation recording | SPA MVP Increment A definition, implementation, terminology, field validation |
| manual questions and evidence attachment | approved Question Pattern subset, Evidence rules, provenance, privacy |
| investigation summaries and handoff | explainability contract, reviewer package, case visibility governance |
| field/offline continuity | synchronization/conflict/provenance design, safety and field validation |

## Human Review

| Capability | Prerequisites |
|---|---|
| Review Request, return, disagreement, escalation | Human Review Operating Model and qualified reviewer capacity |
| provisional candidates and differential comparison | crop Vertical Slice, governed knowledge, evidence and non-ranking controls |
| reviewed/unresolved findings | identifiable reviewer, review provenance, visibility and authority rules |
| Management Options eligibility | separate scientific, regulatory, safety, Product Knowledge, and Human Review governance |

## Field Pilot

| Capability | Prerequisites |
|---|---|
| real case capture and participant operation | Field Validation Plan, consent, sampling, privacy/location handling, stops/rollback |
| images and contextual evidence | capture rules, custody, quality/relevance separation, safe field practice |
| Decision, Action, follow-up, Outcome | SPA Increment C boundaries, ownership, terminology, historical integrity |
| Learning Candidate nomination | Knowledge Review pipeline and explicit no-promotion controls |

## Enterprise

| Capability | Prerequisites |
|---|---|
| Service Provider/Dealer/Crop Advisor experiences | validated persona needs, visibility, authority and non-advice boundaries |
| multi-crop knowledge packages | independently governed packages, scientific review, localization, release |
| enterprise administration and oversight | organization/identity/privacy/audit governance without scientific-authority override |
| analytics and cross-case learning | aggregation, consent, bias, provenance, metric and Human Review governance |
| governed image or AI assistance | authorized datasets/evaluation, explanations, uncertainty, advisory-only behavior, human confirmation |

## Permanently Excluded Without New Authority

Agricultural chatbot behavior, automatic Diagnosis, pesticide or product
Recommendation, expert replacement, hidden ranking, autonomous advice, and
self-learning from unreviewed cases remain prohibited. Future scope cannot enter
Alpha informally through search, navigation, examples, or content summaries.
