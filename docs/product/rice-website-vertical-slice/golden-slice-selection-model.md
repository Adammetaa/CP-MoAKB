# Golden Slice Selection Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Selection Objective

Selection chooses the smallest eligible set that exercises the full Website
Knowledge Architecture. It does not maximize biological coverage, popularity,
commercial value, or apparent completeness.

## Priorities

| Priority | Selection evidence |
|---|---|
| architectural coverage | candidate fills a required Page/View/Relationship role and exposes boundary cases |
| explainability | authority, status, origin, limitations, and non-implications can be explained |
| traceability | exact View, Canonical Object, version, provenance, and Source paths exist |
| canonical readiness | required objects and asserted Relationships are governed and version-identifiable |
| publication eligibility | applicable rights, privacy, security, lifecycle, and publication decisions permit the purpose |
| review readiness | scientific, knowledge, language, representation, and composition review evidence is available |

## Selection Roles

Select exactly one eligible representative for each Disease, Insect, and Weed
detail role, plus the minimum eligible Views needed for Rice Overview, Growth
Stage, Observation, Evidence, Source, Related Knowledge, Management reference,
and Mode of Action reference coverage.

## Selection Process

1. Confirm the role and architecture capability to test.
2. Identify candidates from governed canonical inventories without creating content.
3. reject candidates with unknown identity, provenance, authority, version,
   review, rights, or publication eligibility.
4. Verify asserted Relationships and View compatibility.
5. Record selection rationale, exclusions, limitations, and approval evidence.

## Non-selection Meaning

An item not selected is not scientifically inferior, irrelevant, absent, or
ineligible. Selection demonstrates architecture only and establishes no rank,
coverage claim, recommendation, or precedent for future slices.
