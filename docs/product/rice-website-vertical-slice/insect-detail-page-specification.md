# Insect Detail Page Specification

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Specify the same governed detail architecture for exactly one eligible Insect
role without selecting, naming, describing, or authoring an insect.

## Page Composition

The conceptual Page may contain Identity, Terminology, Observation,
Relationship, Evidence, Source, Review, Related Knowledge, and Limitation Views.
Sections separate identity and scope, neutral observations, evidence and sources,
asserted Relationships, governance, references, and limitations.

Each placement records Page and Section purpose, Knowledge View identity and
version, exact Canonical Object references and versions, provenance, authority,
Review State, publication state, omissions, and limitations.

## Scientific Boundary

Observation remains Observation. Evidence remains Evidence. Neither presence nor
co-occurrence establishes identity, cause, damage attribution, lifecycle stage,
applicability, Diagnosis, threshold, priority, or Recommendation. Page sequence
and Related Knowledge do not create canonical Relationships.

## Reference Boundary

Eligible Management Knowledge and Mode of Action may appear as references only.
They cannot recommend, select, prioritize, decide, or imply a product or
resistance action.

## Exclusions

No biological content, identification key, image classification, monitoring
threshold, treatment advice, product catalogue, active investigation, UI, API,
or Runtime behavior is defined.
