# Evidence, Source, and Observation Views

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Layer Separation

| View | Must present | Must never become or imply |
|---|---|---|
| Observation View | bounded observation identity, observer/source, context, method, result, uncertainty, provenance, version, and status | Evidence, Claim, cause, Diagnosis, or Recommendation |
| Evidence View | exact Evidence identity, scoped relevance/use, linked Source, authority, Review, limitations, provenance, and version | Claim, universal proof, Diagnosis, or automatic applicability |
| Source View | identity, authority context, version, publication/custody/rights status, provenance, citation, and limitations | interpretation, credibility score, Evidence relevance, or endorsement |

Observation remains Observation only. Presentation or review cannot promote it.
Evidence remains Evidence and never becomes a Claim through composition.

## Evidence Explanation Chain

> Evidence -> Source -> Authority -> Review -> Limitations

Each link is an exact governed reference. The chain explains scope and status; it
does not establish truth beyond the Evidence object's authorized meaning.

## Section Composition

Pages may place these Views in separate or coordinated Sections when identities
and provenance remain distinct. A summary supported by several Views traces to
each one and discloses omissions or conflicts. Adjacency does not create a Claim.

## Visibility

Review-pending, internal, restricted, not-published, deprecated, superseded, or
otherwise ineligible Views follow their governing visibility rules. Absence from
a public Page is not negative scientific evidence.

## Prohibitions

No scoring, ranking, causal interpretation, automated Evidence promotion,
Diagnosis, Recommendation, product selection, or Runtime behavior is authorized.
