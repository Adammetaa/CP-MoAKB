# Weed Detail Page Specification

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Specify the same governed detail architecture for exactly one eligible Weed role
without selecting, naming, describing, or authoring a weed.

## Page Composition

The conceptual Page may contain Identity, Terminology, Observation,
Relationship, Evidence, Source, Review, Related Knowledge, and Limitation Views.
Required Sections distinguish identity and terminology, bounded observations,
Evidence and Sources, asserted Relationships, review and provenance, references,
and limitations.

All Views must be compatible and eligible for the audience and publication
context. Every Section preserves exact View and Canonical Object identities,
versions, authority, Review State, publication state, provenance, and limitations.

## Scientific Boundary

Observation remains Observation and Evidence remains Evidence. Composition does
not infer identity, competition, impact, growth-stage association, causality,
applicability, Diagnosis, ranking, threshold, or Recommendation. Editorial
navigation is visibly distinct from canonical Relationships.

## Reference Boundary

Management Knowledge and Mode of Action remain references only. No reference may
recommend, select, prioritize, decide, or imply treatment, product, or resistance action.

## Exclusions

No biological descriptions, identification keys, image intelligence, management
advice, product catalogue, active investigation, implementation, or Runtime behavior.
