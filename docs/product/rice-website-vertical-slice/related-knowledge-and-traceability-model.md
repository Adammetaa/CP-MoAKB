# Related Knowledge and Traceability Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Related Knowledge

Related Knowledge presents only canonically asserted Relationships or explicitly
identified editorial navigation. It never infers a new Relationship from shared
terms, adjacency, co-occurrence, page sequence, user behavior, or graph paths.

An asserted Relationship presentation retains source and target identities,
type, direction, basis, scope, provenance, authority, Review State, publication
eligibility, version, and limitations. Editorial navigation cannot borrow those semantics.

## Required Traceability

Every material Page Section preserves:

> Section -> Knowledge View and Representation Version -> Canonical Object
> identity and version -> Canonical Knowledge provenance

No traceability loss is permitted. Page titles, summaries, cards, references,
and Related Knowledge placements retain or expose the same deterministic path.

## Management Knowledge Reference

Management Knowledge remains reference only. A reference may present eligible
identity, scope, authority, review, publication, Source, version, provenance, and
limitations. It must never recommend, select, prioritize, decide, rank, or imply
case-specific applicability, treatment, product, timing, or action.

## Explainability

Each Section explains why its Views are included, whether links are canonical or
editorial, where content originated, who holds authority, what review occurred,
which versions are shown, what is omitted, and what the composition does not imply.

## Historical and Restricted Content

Superseded or deprecated content retains historical identity and replacement
context. Restricted or absent content is not exposed through links, counts, or
summaries and is not represented as scientific nonexistence.
