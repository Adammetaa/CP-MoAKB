# Program Purpose and Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Program Governance provides one operating model for Architecture, Knowledge,
Product, Program Management, Scientific Authority, Human Review, Field
Validation, Runtime Engineering, Release/Publication, and long-term evolution.
It enables parallel work while preserving domain accountability and traceability
from Vision through Release and Learning.

## Responsibilities

It coordinates offices; governs Critical Path, dependencies, risks, gates, and
major milestones; prevents scope creep and premature implementation; prevents
unreviewed Knowledge entering Product or Runtime; prevents Product from
overriding science and Runtime convenience from weakening Architecture; and
governs expansion from SPA MVP toward multi-crop, Explainable AI, and enterprise capability.

## Explicit Exclusions

Program Governance does not author scientific content, replace Architecture or
Product authority, replace Scientific or Human Review, implement software,
approve evidence outside assigned authority, waive Design Freeze or Publication
Boundary, or treat schedule pressure as authority to bypass a gate.

## Domain Distinctions

- **Program Governance:** defines cross-domain coordination, gates, escalation,
  residual-risk and lifecycle accountability.
- **Program Management:** maintains roadmap, dependencies, sequencing, evidence,
  status, and decision preparation without approving domain readiness.
- **Architecture Governance:** protects architecture consistency and boundaries.
- **Knowledge Governance:** controls knowledge architecture, content, lifecycle,
  terminology, and packages.
- **Product Governance:** controls vision, users, journeys, scope, increments, and acceptance.
- **Scientific Governance:** controls source/evidence authority and scientific interpretation.
- **Human Review Governance:** controls reviewer qualification, case-review process, and provenance.
- **Field Validation Governance:** controls pilot design, sampling, evidence, stops, and debriefs.
- **Release Governance:** controls eligibility, release types, publication,
  withdrawal, deprecation, supersession, and retirement.

## Separation of Authority

Coordination never means approval. An artifact may need several mandatory
concurrences, but each Office approves only its domain. Final Program Owner
accountability is distinct from Office concurrence and Release Office execution.
Missing scientific, privacy, legal, regulatory, Design Freeze, Human Review, or
Publication authority is blocking and cannot receive a schedule waiver.

## Audit Principle

Every cross-domain decision preserves initiator, scope, evidence, consulted and
mandatory offices, objections, conditions, approver, executor, dependency/risk
effects, version, date, and return or escalation path.
