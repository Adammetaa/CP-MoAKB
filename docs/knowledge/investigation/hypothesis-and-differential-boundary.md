# Hypothesis and Differential Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Hypothesis Candidate

A Hypothesis Candidate is a provisional, explicitly authored explanation that
is traceable to recorded Observations and governed Knowledge. It remains open to
challenge and may coexist with competing candidates. It is never inferred
automatically and is not equivalent to truth or Diagnosis.

Authorship must disclose the bounded explanatory proposition, observation and
knowledge basis, limitations, plausible alternatives, and potential comparison
criteria. A terminology match, local name, image similarity, repeated pattern,
or model output cannot author or confirm a candidate.

## Differential Set

A Differential Set contains two or more plausible Hypothesis Candidates where
appropriate. It preserves competing explanations and records explicit Comparison
Criteria plus supporting, challenging, missing, and conflicting evidence. It
does not rank, score, sort by plausibility, select, eliminate, or diagnose
automatically. Human review is required.

If only one candidate is currently authored, the case must not disguise it as a
differential. Reviewers should examine whether alternatives were omitted, while
recognizing that the architecture does not automatically generate them.

## Comparison Criteria

A criterion states a relevant distinction that can be examined across
candidates. Its knowledge basis, applicability, Evidence Needs, and limitations
remain explicit. Evidence is assessed per criterion and candidate; support for
one criterion is not support for every aspect of a candidate.

Comparisons preserve at least four distinct conditions:

- **supporting:** scoped evidence is consistent with a candidate or criterion;
- **challenging:** scoped evidence bears against it;
- **missing:** relevant evidence has not been assessed or remains unavailable; and
- **conflicting:** relevant assessed material gives incompatible indications.

None is a numeric score or automatic decision rule.

## Retention and Rejection

Candidates may remain unresolved, be revised, or be explicitly rejected for a
bounded purpose after human review. Rejection requires rationale and does not
erase history or establish a competing candidate as true. New material may
justify reopening comparison under authorized review.

## Diagnosis Boundary

A reviewed differential remains an investigation artifact. Even a candidate
with substantial support is not a Diagnosis. Any future diagnostic act requires
separate authority, authorship, inputs, review, and governance outside this
ontology. No unexplained score, ordering, completion percentage, or interface
highlight may substitute for that act.

## Localization Boundary

Thai-first candidate labels and explanations may be used with translation
provenance and explicit English technical alignment. Local or vernacular names
must not be treated as confirmed scientific identity, and translation similarity
must not merge candidates.
