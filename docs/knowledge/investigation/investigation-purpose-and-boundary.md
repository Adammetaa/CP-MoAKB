# Investigation Purpose and Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Investigation gives human reviewers a governed structure for asking what is
known, what remains unresolved, what evidence would matter, and which explicitly
authored provisional explanations merit comparison. Its output is a reviewable
account of inquiry, not a diagnosis or recommendation.

## Inclusion Boundary

Investigation may organize:

- recorded Observations and their declared limitations;
- Information States and Information Gaps;
- bounded Investigation Focuses and neutral Investigation Questions;
- Evidence Needs distinct from later Evidence Objects;
- explicitly authored Hypothesis Candidates and Differential Sets;
- Comparison Criteria and supporting, challenging, missing, or conflicting
  evidence assessments; and
- Investigation Findings and Review States.

The structure begins only after an Observation exists. It may refer back to the
Observation and forward to possible human review, but it does not change the
Observation or pre-author downstream conclusions.

## Exclusion Boundary

Investigation does not establish cause, identity confirmation, severity,
diagnosis, treatment need, regulatory meaning, safety, efficacy,
recommendation, decision, action, or outcome. It does not generate hypotheses,
score candidates, select winners, infer missing facts, or promote findings.

An Investigation Question is not an Observation, Claim, Evidence, Hypothesis,
Diagnosis, or Recommendation. An Evidence Need is not an Evidence Object. A
Hypothesis Candidate is neither truth nor Diagnosis. An Investigation Finding
does not automatically become a Claim, Diagnosis, Recommendation, or Decision.

## Permanent Separation

Observation, Evidence, Knowledge, Hypothesis, Diagnosis, Recommendation,
Decision, Action, and Outcome are independently governed layers. A link between
them records an asserted relationship; it does not transfer authority or status.
Review approval within an Investigation changes only its declared Review State.

## Investigation Versus Observation

Observation records a bounded report, perception, measurement, count, image, or
other capture. Investigation organizes questions about such records. A question
about lesion origin does not become an observation that an origin exists, and a
missing record does not become a negative observation.

## Investigation Versus Knowledge and Evidence

Knowledge may inform why a question or comparison is relevant, but knowledge
does not determine the case outcome. Evidence is separately evaluated for a
precise use; merely requesting or locating material does not make it Evidence.
Every use must retain traceability to the relevant Observation, knowledge basis,
question, gap, criterion, and human review.

## Localization and Extension

The architecture permits Thai-first presentation with explicit language,
translation provenance, and independently reviewable English technical
alignment. A local name must not be treated as confirmed scientific identity.
Multi-crop extensions may supply governed terminology and knowledge references
without changing neutral classes or embedding a crop in the structure.

## Future Interfaces

Future SPA or Agronomist workflows may display gaps, ask questions, collect
candidate evidence, and support review. Presentation order, completion signals,
or interface defaults must not assert inference, ranking, mandatory progression,
or automatic promotion.
