# Information State and Gap Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Information State

An Information State records the epistemic condition of specified information
within a bounded case. It must name the information in scope, basis for the
assessment, author or reviewer, and relevant context. States are not synonyms,
truth probabilities, or stages in a mandatory sequence.

| State | Meaning | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| known | accepted as established for the bounded investigative purpose on a stated basis | universally true or diagnosed |
| reported | communicated by an attributable source but not necessarily directly observed by the recorder | verified or measured |
| directly observed | perceived or captured directly under stated conditions | causal, complete, or general |
| measured | produced by a declared measurement method with units and limitations | exact beyond method limits |
| not observed | sought under stated conditions but not detected | absent in all contexts |
| not recorded | no record exists in the reviewed material | not observed or absent |
| unknown | the value or answer is not known | negative, unavailable, or false |
| unavailable | relevant information cannot currently be accessed or obtained | nonexistent or adverse |
| conflicting | relevant accounts or assessments are incompatible | resolved by majority or recency |
| not applicable | the declared requirement does not apply in the bounded context, with rationale | unknown or omitted |
| pending review | information awaits an identified human review | accepted, rejected, or true |
| rejected | a reviewer rejected specified information for a stated purpose and rationale | erased, disproven in every context, or forbidden to retain |

“Known” must still disclose its basis and purpose. “Not observed” requires a
bounded attempt and detection context. “Not recorded” expresses documentation
status only. Conflicting information preserves all relevant accounts until an
authorized human review records a scoped disposition.

## Information Gap

An Information Gap represents a missing or unresolved requirement for the
investigation. It identifies what is needed, why it matters to a focus or
comparison, and which Information State demonstrates the unresolved condition.
It may arise from unknown, unavailable, conflicting, not recorded, pending
review, method-limited, or insufficiently localized information.

A gap does not assert diagnosis, cause, severity, recommendation, treatment
need, or regulatory meaning. It does not imply that the information exists, that
it can be obtained, or that resolving it will decide the case.

## Gap Handling

A human author may refine a gap, raise one or more neutral questions, describe
Evidence Needs, record attempts, or state that the gap remains unresolved. A gap
is addressed only for a declared purpose and basis. “Addressed” is not the same
as “answer known,” and case closure may preserve open gaps.

## Critical Distinctions

- No record of lesion origin is `not recorded`, not `not observed`.
- A failed attempt to see an origin under one image angle may be `not observed`
  for that bounded review, not proof that no origin exists.
- Incompatible location reports are `conflicting`, not averaged into “known.”
- Material awaiting an agronomist is `pending review`, not implicitly accepted.
- Absence of requested evidence remains a gap; it is not evidence of absence.

## Review

Reviewers verify state choice, scope, basis, state history, and whether any gap
was silently turned into a negative observation. Localization must preserve all
distinctions; Thai labels may not merge `unknown`, `unavailable`, `not recorded`,
or `not observed` into a single undifferentiated state.
