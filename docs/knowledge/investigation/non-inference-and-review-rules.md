# Non-inference and Review Rules

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Mandatory Non-inference Rules

1. Observation must remain distinct from Investigation Question.
2. Missing information must remain explicit and must not become a negative Observation.
3. Information States must not be merged or treated as a confidence scale.
4. Investigation Questions must not presuppose or force a preferred diagnosis.
5. Evidence Need must remain distinct from Evidence Object.
6. Material collection must not automatically satisfy a need or establish Evidence.
7. Hypothesis Candidates must be explicitly authored and never automatically generated.
8. Hypothesis Candidate must remain distinct from truth and Diagnosis.
9. Differential comparison must preserve competing explanations and adverse material.
10. Candidate ordering, counts, scores, colors, or interface emphasis must not rank or select.
11. Absence of evidence must not be treated as evidence of absence.
12. A local name must not confirm scientific identity.
13. Investigation Finding must not automatically become Claim, Diagnosis,
    Recommendation, or Decision.
14. Review approval must not promote content across epistemic layers.
15. No graph path or relationship composition licenses inference.

## Human Review Requirements

An accountable reviewer checks scope, authorship, provenance, state distinctions,
question neutrality, evidence relevance, candidate basis, alternative
preservation, comparison criteria, conflicting material, findings, and residual
gaps. Review records include rationale, limitations, dissent, recusals where
relevant, and the precise purpose of any acceptance or rejection.

Human review is substantive, not a completion checkbox. A reviewer may return an
entity for revision, preserve conflict, reject an interpretation, or leave a gap
unresolved. Silence, timeout, interface progression, or case closure is not approval.

## Determinism and Explainability

Given the same authored content and declared review rules, presentation and
descriptive classification should be reproducible. Determinism does not mean
automated reasoning. Every displayed conclusion must be reconstructable from
explicit assertions and reviews; hidden scores, model confidence, implicit
defaults, and undocumented transformations are prohibited.

## Review Failure Conditions

Review fails when a question encodes a cause, a gap is represented as absence,
a need is labeled as evidence, a candidate is presented as Diagnosis, a
differential hides a competitor or adverse material, a finding crosses into
recommendation, or crop-specific assumptions become structural. Ambiguity and
conflict remain visible until an authorized resolution is recorded.

## Future Workflow Boundary

Future systems may assist navigation, transcription, localization, and display.
They may not infer states, create candidates, rank differentials, approve
findings, or promote layers. Any later proposal for such behavior requires
separate governance and must not be read into this architecture.
