# Evidence Need Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

An Evidence Need describes what evidence would be relevant to resolving an
Information Gap or comparing Hypothesis Candidates. It makes the requested
relevance, scope, and context reviewable before material is collected or assessed.

## Need Versus Evidence Object

An Evidence Need is prospective and requirement-oriented. An Evidence Object is
a separately governed assessment of exact material for a precise Claim or use.
Describing a need does not create evidence. Obtaining an image, report,
measurement, or specimen does not automatically satisfy the need or convert the
material into an Evidence Object.

## Conceptual Content

A well-bounded need explains:

- the Information Gap, Investigation Question, candidate, or criterion it concerns;
- what observable, measurable, documentary, or comparative material is relevant;
- required temporal, spatial, specimen, method, provenance, and quality context;
- what limitations would prevent resolution;
- whether supporting, challenging, missing, or conflicting material is relevant; and
- which human reviewer assesses fitness for the stated purpose.

These are semantic requirements, not schema fields or collection instructions.

## Permitted Need Categories

Needs may concern clarification, localization, measurement, count, temporal
history, spatial distribution, specimen capture, management history,
environmental context, comparison, or verification. Categories do not determine
the method, imply availability, or carry diagnostic meaning.

## Satisfaction Boundary

Material may be nominated against a need, then separately assessed. A human
reviewer may determine that it addresses the need fully, partly, not at all, or
conflicts with other material, always for a bounded purpose. Satisfaction of a
need does not prove a Hypothesis Candidate, resolve the Differential Set, or
authorize Diagnosis or Recommendation.

## Example Boundary

For the gap “lesion initiation point not recorded,” a relevant need could be “a
close image showing lesion origin and relationship to leaf veins.” This states
what view would matter. It does not assert that the image exists, that its
contents are Evidence, or that vein relationship identifies a cause.

## Review and Extension

Reviewers test relevance, neutrality, feasibility, proportionality, provenance
expectations, and whether the need is leading toward a preferred diagnosis.
Future Agronomist or SPA experiences may present and track needs but may not
auto-create Evidence Objects, infer satisfaction, or turn collection into a
recommendation. Crop-specific methods may be referenced under separate
authority; they do not alter the crop-independent model.
