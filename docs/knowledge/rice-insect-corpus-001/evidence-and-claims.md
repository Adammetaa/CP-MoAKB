## General physiology Evidence

- `EV-RIC-MECH-001/v1`: Chapman fourth edition, PDF p.31, chapter 2,
  “Mouthparts and feeding”, scoped paraphrase: mouthpart form relates to diet and
  includes basic biting/chewing solid-food and fluid-sucking forms.
- `EV-RIC-MECH-002/v1`: Chapman PDF pp.34-37, sections 2.1.2-2.1.4, scoped
  paraphrase: larval Lepidoptera and Coleoptera commonly have biting/chewing
  mouthparts; Hemiptera exemplifies piercing/sucking structures; mouthpart sensilla
  participate in feeding-related perception.
- `CL-RIC-MECH-001/v1` and `CL-RIC-MECH-002/v1` remain general insect physiology.
  They do not independently assert the feeding mechanism of any named rice pest.

## Regulatory Evidence

`EV-RIC-REG-001/v1` cites the registry title/header and column definitions on PDF
p.1. It supports only that the artifact is a dated registration-record snapshot.
No pest, crop, efficacy, dose, or selection Claim is produced from it.
