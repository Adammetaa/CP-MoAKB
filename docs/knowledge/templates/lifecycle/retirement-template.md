# Retirement Template

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Document the governed end of active use while retaining archival history.
## Scope
Retirement reason, affected scope, archival location, dependencies, reviews, and audit.
## Out of Scope
Retirement MUST NOT erase identity, evidence, disagreement, publication history, or automatically supersede.
## Authority
Governed by [Template Governance](../template-governance.md), KAS-007, Editorial lifecycle guide, Review Framework, and KGS-003/005/006.
## When to Use
When an identity/version should no longer participate in active governed use.
## Who Completes It
Managing Editor proposes; affected specialists/governance decide; archive and release roles execute only authorized actions.
## Required Inputs
Identity/version, retirement evidence, dependency/impact analysis, archival plan, reviews, and authority.

## Completion Rules
Retired material MUST remain archived, identifiable, traceable, and visibly inactive.
## Prohibited Content
Destructive deletion, identity reuse, hidden dependency removal, implicit successor, or publication rewrite.
## Review Requirements
Affected specialist, governance, archive, rights, and publication reviews apply by state.
## Failure Modes
No archive plan, lost evidence, active references left unexplained, or unauthorized retirement.
## Example
A fictional version MAY retire from active use while remaining historically accessible.
## Non-example
Deleting its files and audit history MUST NOT pass.
## Audit and Retention
Retain all versions, evidence, decisions, dependencies, notices, archive location, and reinstatement attempts.
## Change Control
Template changes require lifecycle, archival, KGS, and publication-boundary analysis.
