# Fictional Incomplete and Blocked Package

Status: Fictional, illustrative, blocked
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Show why template presence is insufficient when required evidence, rights, language, relationships, and independence are missing.
## Scope
Synthetic candidate `fictional-template-object-umbra`, version 1.
## Out of Scope
No real crop, disease, insect, chemical, authority, source, regulation, diagnosis, recommendation, or agricultural assertion appears.
## Authority
Illustrative only under [Template Governance](../template-governance.md) and Review Framework.
## When to Use
Use to train reviewers to identify incomplete packages and unresolved blockers.
## Who Completes It
The example is maintained as documentation; fictional roles illustrate required reassignment.
## Required Inputs
The deliberately deficient package contains a title-only invented source, an
unlocated assertion, ambiguous invented terms, an unsupported high-risk relationship,
unknown image rights, and an Author assigned as their own Scientific Reviewer.
## Template Fields

| Defect | Required classification/effect | Required response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing source identity/version | Governance Blocker and Evidence Gap | Establish governed identity/version or reject source use |
| Rights unknown for fictional image | Rights Blocker | Exclude image or obtain verified permission |
| No exact evidence locator/context | Evidence Gap | Supply verifiable fictional evidence or narrow/remove assertion |
| Two ambiguous preferred-term proposals | Conflict / Clarification Required | Preserve both, obtain competent Terminology Review |
| Unsupported `causes` relationship | Blocking high-risk relationship | Remove or provide scoped evidence and competent Scientific/Ontology Review |
| Author assigned as reviewer | Conflict of interest | Recuse and appoint independent approved reviewer |
| Open blockers | Final gate failure | Resolve independently before acceptance review |

## Completion Rules
The package remains incomplete and blocked. Filling headings without supported content MUST NOT change that disposition.
## Prohibited Content
Invented source details to fill blanks, inferred rights, forced term consensus, numeric confidence, majority override, diagnosis, or advice.
## Review Requirements
Evidence, Terminology, Scientific, Ontology, Rights, and Governance Reviews remain incomplete or blocked.
## Failure Modes
Author self-closure, deleting adverse findings, adding “90% confidence,” or calling the package ready.
## Example
Decision: return for revision, preserve all findings, recuse the Author-reviewer, and keep `not_published`.
## Non-example
Five completed headings outvoting one Rights Blocker MUST NOT pass.
## Audit and Retention
Retain the incomplete version, every finding, rejected response, recusal, replacement, revision, and future verification.
## Change Control
A resubmission requires a new package version and affected reviews; this example remains fictional.
