# Review Completion Template

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Prove one required review completed properly for one fixed version.
## Scope
Assignment, competence, independence, inputs, findings, revisions, decision, and audit evidence.
## Out of Scope
Completion MUST NOT imply acceptance, scientific validity, readiness, or publication.
## Authority
Governed by [Template Governance](../template-governance.md), Review Framework completion record, and KGS-006.
## When to Use
After a reviewer issues a disposition and all required review actions are recorded.
## Who Completes It
Assigned reviewer attests; Managing Editor verifies completeness; Governance Reviewer may audit.
## Required Inputs
Candidate/version, assignment, competence, COI assessment, decision, finding log, responses, and closure evidence.

## Completion Rules
Missing assignment, competence, declaration, input version, decision, or finding reconciliation means incomplete.
## Prohibited Content
Score, unversioned scope, recused-reviewer completion, hidden blocker, machine signature requirement, or publication implication.
## Review Requirements
Managing Editor verifies completeness; Governance Review addresses contested or high-impact completion.
## Failure Modes
Wrong version, copied attestation, open blocker omitted, or material revision not re-reviewed.
## Example
A fictional deferred review MAY be complete when reasons and reopening condition are fully recorded.
## Non-example
“Review complete” without findings and decision MUST NOT pass.
## Audit and Retention
Retain assignment, competence, declaration, inputs, findings, decisions, attestation, corrections, and supersession.
## Change Control
Field changes require KGS-006 and Review Framework analysis.
