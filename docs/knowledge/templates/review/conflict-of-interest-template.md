# Conflict-of-Interest Declaration Template

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Record conflict disclosure, assessment, mitigation, recusal, and replacement before review.
## Scope
Financial, organizational, authorship, supervisory, competitive, personal, regulatory, and publication conflicts.
## Out of Scope
This template MUST NOT collect unnecessary personal data or decide competence.
## Authority
Governed by [Template Governance](../template-governance.md), Review Framework independence controls, and KGS-002/003.
## When to Use
Before substantive review and whenever circumstances change.
## Who Completes It
Reviewer declares; authorized independent assessor decides mitigation/recusal; Managing Editor arranges replacement.
## Required Inputs
Reviewer identity, assignment, candidate/version, conflict categories, relevant facts, and assessment authority.

## Completion Rules
Every category needs none known, disclosed, or uncertain. No declaration means no review.
## Prohibited Content
Silent conflicts, unnecessary personal data, reviewer self-ranking, or retroactive clearance.
## Review Requirements
Independent assessment is mandatory; material unmitigated conflict requires recusal and replacement.
## Failure Modes
Blank category, vague “none,” late disclosure, token mitigation, or erased recusal history.
## Example
A fictional candidate author declares authorship conflict and is replaced for independent closure.
## Non-example
“I can be objective” MUST NOT substitute for assessment.
## Audit and Retention
Retain declaration, necessary facts, assessment, mitigation, recusal, replacement, updates, and privacy basis.
## Change Control
Fields require KGS, independence, privacy, and Review Framework analysis.
