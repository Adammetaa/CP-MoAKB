## Claim records

`CL-RWC-001-I/v1` through `CL-RWC-008-I/v1` assert only the source-named Thai
common identity. `CL-RWC-001-G/v1` through `CL-RWC-008-G/v1` assign only the
source-supported weed group. `CL-RWC-001-C/v1` through `CL-RWC-008-C/v1` state
only that the manual lists the subject among weeds in rice fields. Identity/group
Claims resolve to `EV-RWC-002/v1`, `EV-RWC-003/v1`, or `EV-RWC-004/v1`; every
rice-context Claim resolves to `EV-RWC-004/v1`.

in rice including หญ้าข้าวนก and หญ้าดอกขาว (`EV-RWC-006/v1`). It is not projected
as current registration, efficacy guarantee, product or case Recommendation.
