# Reconciliation, Review, and Unresolved Issues

## Review

Architecture and scientific-boundary review accepted the eight subjects for
bounded internal use. The single scientific Source is internally complementary
across sections: section 3 supplies group cues, section 5 supplies rice-field
occurrence, sections 6-10 supply general management and herbicide context.
The registration Source has a different administrative scope and is not merged.

No conflicting evidence was found because no second eligible weed-science Source
was available. The owner workbook was inspected but contains insect records only;
it is not scientific weed Evidence. HRAC official material was not available, so
2019 manual families/modes are not relabeled as current HRAC groups.

## Unresolved Issues

- `UI-RWC-001/v1`: scientific identities, English names, synonyms and common-name
  ambiguity require a qualified taxonomic/weed-identification Source.
- `UI-RWC-002/v1`: species-level morphology and pairwise look-alike distinctions
  are unsupported; only group-level cues are exposed.
- `UI-RWC-003/v1`: rice stage, weed emergence timing, ecology, life history and
  yield consequences are unsupported for individual subjects.
- `UI-RWC-004/v1`: current rice/weed/use-pattern registration, efficacy, crop
  safety, rate and timing require exact current records and labels.
- `UI-RWC-005/v1`: resistance status and HRAC mapping require qualified official
  Evidence; failed control alone never resolves this issue.

## Architecture boundary

No new architecture, schema, parser, registry, Runtime Core, API, backend, AI,
database, review framework, Diagnosis, Recommendation, product ranking or
commercial scoring was created. Design Freeze remains unchanged. Source PDFs and
copyrighted content remain local and are not reproduced.
