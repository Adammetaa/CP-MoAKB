# Relationships, Surveillance Implications, and Unresolved Issues

## Relationship governance

Environmental/transmission records are projections of existing accepted Claims
and Evidence, not a new object family. Each relationship retains one role:

- `transmitted-by`: disease → vector; never vector → infection confirmation.
- `spread-by`: subject → explicitly Source-stated pathway.
- `influenced-by`: subject → favorable/development context; never transmission.
- `associated-with-stage`: subject → temporal crop context.
- `observation-context`: subject → field condition; never causation.

Field-pattern observations may prompt additional inspection, but cannot diagnose
a cause. Nearby-field surveillance is justified only as an investigation action
when the subject-specific pathway and observable evidence are both present.

Case observation ≠ Canonical Knowledge. Case cluster ≠ confirmed outbreak.
Nearby Case ≠ transmission event.

## Quantitative controls

No numerical weather threshold and no quantitative dispersal distance is
accepted in this corpus. No studies are averaged. No experimental distance is
converted into an operational radius. Future weather inputs must preserve source
type and resolution: station, gridded dataset, forecast model, local sensor, or
manual field report are not interchangeable and are not exact field measures.

## Grouped unresolved issues

| ID | Scope | Resolution needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `UI-ETS-001/v1` | quantitative dispersal evidence | qualified subject- and mechanism-specific distance studies, with method, geography, time, and environment |
| `UI-ETS-002/v1` | environmental thresholds | authoritative or peer-reviewed thresholds with units, locator, context, and Thai-field applicability review |
| `UI-ETS-003/v1` | vector movement | vector-specific movement/activity evidence without equating presence with infection |
| `UI-ETS-004/v1` | Thai-local validation | review of whether non-local epidemiological evidence transfers to Thai rice systems |
| `UI-ETS-005/v1` | weather resolution | governance for station, grid, forecast, sensor, and manual observations before compatibility logic |

No issue blocks qualitative supported records. Live weather, nearby-case lookup,
outbreak prediction, risk scoring, Diagnosis, and Recommendation remain absent.
