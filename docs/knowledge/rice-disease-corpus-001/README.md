# Rice Disease Corpus Population Wave

Status: Accepted for bounded internal composition; not published

This corpus registers two supplied rice-disease Sources through the existing
Source intake model and materializes 16 canonical disease subjects. Ten subjects
have multi-Source support; six have single-Source coverage. Existing identities
`CO-RDW1-001/v1` (โรคใบสีส้มของข้าว) and `CO-RDW1-003/v1` (โรคจู๋ของข้าว) are
reused rather than duplicated.

Every disease has three scoped Claim responsibilities: identity, cause or
etiologic context, and observation/biology context. Unsupported details remain
absent. Chemical material is not converted into present registration, product
selection, field Diagnosis, or Recommendation.
