# Terminology Authoring

Terminology Candidates assert labels about an exact Concept identity. Supported
term roles include accepted term, preferred term, synonym, field term, deprecated
term, and translation, each with language, jurisdiction/community, authority,
source, version, scope, ambiguity, and status.

Language, spelling, scientific name, translation, synonymy, or preferred display
never equals Concept identity. Similar labels do not prove equivalent Concepts.
Translation must preserve meaning and record translator/reviewer responsibility.

Unsupported or contested labels remain candidate, ambiguous, deprecated, or
unresolved; editorial preference cannot manufacture terminology authority.
