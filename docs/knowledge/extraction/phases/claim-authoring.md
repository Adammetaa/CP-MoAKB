# Claim Authoring

One reviewed Evidence item may produce zero, one, or multiple Claim Candidates
when each represents one separately reviewable proposition. Every Claim declares
subject/context, exact wording, scope, exclusions, applicability, time,
jurisdiction, Evidence links, uncertainty, limitations, and candidate status.

Claims do not exceed the Evidence, universalize a bounded result, hide adverse
Evidence, combine independent propositions, or convert correlation into cause.
No Diagnosis or Recommendation may be authored.

Claim Review evaluates proposition fidelity, Evidence fit, scope, conflicts,
authority, risk, and language. Approval is explicit and does not create a Concept.
