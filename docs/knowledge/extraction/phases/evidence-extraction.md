# Evidence Extraction

An Evidence Candidate is a bounded, claim-specific interpretation of an exact
Source version and Relevant Passage. It records Section/passage locator, Citation,
context, method, scope, population, jurisdiction/time, support/contradiction/
limitation role, uncertainty, rights, translation, omissions, and limitations.

A Source title, abstract alone, copied conclusion, author reputation, search
snippet, chat answer, unlocated paraphrase, or entire document is not Evidence.
Quotations are necessary, bounded, exact, attributed, and rights-permitted;
otherwise use a faithful independently authored paraphrase.

Evidence Review checks fidelity and provenance. Evidence must never become Claim
automatically, and one passage may yield zero or several distinct candidates.
