# Confidence Notes

Confidence notes are qualitative author/reviewer explanations of completeness,
interpretive risk, missing context, conflicting material, and limitations. They
do not create numeric confidence, probability, truth score, Evidence level,
acceptance, Diagnosis, or Recommendation.

Notes identify their author role, basis, scope, date, affected exact versions,
and unresolved questions. Model confidence and fluent wording have no canonical
authority. Review dispositions remain separate governed objects.
