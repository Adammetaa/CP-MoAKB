# Ambiguity Handling

Authors record the exact ambiguous wording, plausible interpretations, context,
missing information, affected candidates, materiality, and escalation path.
Multiple interpretations remain separate; the Author does not select the most
convenient or confident-sounding meaning.

Ambiguity may yield no candidate, a scoped candidate with an attached limitation,
or an Unresolved Issue routed to scientific, terminology, ontology, rights, or
governance review. Conflict is preserved rather than averaged or silently resolved.
