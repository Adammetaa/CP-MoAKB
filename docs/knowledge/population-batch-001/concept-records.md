## CO-KPB-001/v1 - Plant disease

- Definition: the bounded meaning carried by `CL-KPB-001/v1`.
- Scope: general plant-disease concept as defined by the fixed Source.
- Exclusions: particular disease identity, observation, cause attribution,

- Evidence: `EV-KPB-001/v1`; Source `P36-S1-A`.

## CO-KPB-002/v1 - Nonliving-cause plant-disease category

- Definition: the source-classified category `โรคพืชที่มีสาเหตุเกิดจากสิ่งไม่มีชีวิต
  หรือเกิดจากความผิดปกติทางสรีรวิทยาของพืชเอง`.
- Scope: one category in the fixed Source classification.
- Exclusions: a specific abiotic factor, observed condition, or case conclusion.
- Evidence: `EV-KPB-002/v1`; Source `P37-S3-A`, item 3.1.

## CO-KPB-003/v1 - Living-cause plant-disease category

- Definition: the source-classified category `โรคพืชที่มีสาเหตุเกิดจากสิ่งมีชีวิต`.
- Scope: one category in the fixed Source classification.
- Exclusions: identity of a particular pathogen, current taxonomic completeness,
  or cause attribution in a field case.
- Evidence: `EV-KPB-002/v1`; Source `P37-S3-A`, item 3.2.

Concept identities remain separate from their labels and from package placement.
