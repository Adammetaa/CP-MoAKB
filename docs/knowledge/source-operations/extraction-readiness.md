# Source Extraction Readiness

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

A Source may enter KES only when all applicable criteria pass:

- exact Source identity and version are fixed;
- official/authorized origin and retrieval provenance are reproducible;
- authority scope is accepted for the intended extraction purpose;
- rights for access, review, citation, quotation and retained treatment are decided;
- integrity is verified or its governed method/disposition is complete;
- language, jurisdiction, publication/time scope and coverage are known;
- conceptual manifest responsibilities are complete;
- catalog registration and availability status are approved;
- stable page/section/passage addressing is possible;
- corrections, retractions, replacements, limitations and blockers are recorded; and
- competent Source, rights and extraction-readiness reviews are complete.

Readiness is purpose-specific and exact-version-specific. It means only that KES
Source Analysis may begin. It does not create a Relevant Passage, Evidence,
Claim, acceptance, Package membership, publication, Diagnosis, or Recommendation.

Any failed criterion blocks the intended extraction and records an owner and
resolution path. No score or majority of criteria can override a mandatory blocker.
