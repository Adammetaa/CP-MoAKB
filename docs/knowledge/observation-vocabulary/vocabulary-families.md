# Observation Vocabulary Families

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This document identifies conceptual families for organizing future observation
terminology without constructing term lists or code systems.

## Family Architecture

### Observable Entity

Terms in this family identify what is directly observed, without inferring a
causal identity. Entity scope MUST distinguish directly perceived identity from
identity inferred from effects.

### Structure and Location

Terms in this family describe where a feature occurs: a structure, surface,
region, boundary, layer, or relative position. Location MUST remain distinct
from cause and diagnosis.

### Property

Terms in this family describe an observable or measurable characteristic such
as shape, size, color, texture, continuity, opacity, or orientation. Property
definitions MUST state the basis of perception or measurement when ambiguity is
material.

### State and Presence

Terms in this family express state, presence, absence, or not-assessed status.
Absence MUST mean absence under the stated observation conditions, not universal
absence.

### Quantity and Count

Terms in this family support direct counts and governed qualitative quantities.
Qualitative expressions MUST disclose their comparison basis and MUST NOT imply
severity, risk, or treatment thresholds unless separately governed outside this
architecture.

### Spatial Distribution

Terms in this family describe arrangement, clustering, dispersion, boundaries,
or relative placement. A distribution pattern MUST NOT name its presumed cause.

### Temporal Pattern

Terms in this family describe observed sequence, persistence, recurrence,
change, or timing. Temporal association MUST NOT be represented as causation or
prediction.

### Visible or Measurable Change

Terms in this family describe change relative to a declared comparison or prior
observation. The reference state and observation interval MUST be reviewable.

### Uncertainty

Terms in this family communicate limitations, ambiguity, indeterminacy, or
observer confidence. They MUST NOT convert subjective confidence into evidence
strength or probability without separate governance.

### Context Reference

Terms in this family identify relevant observation conditions such as method,
instrument, time, environment, crop, organ, growth stage, or sampling frame.
They reference context; they MUST NOT hide context inside a descriptive label.

## Cross-family Use

A description MAY combine terms from multiple families. Each term MUST retain
one reviewable responsibility. Composition MUST NOT create a hidden diagnosis,
hypothesis, recommendation, or evidence claim.

## Extensibility

Future crop or domain extensions MAY specialize these families only after
governed review. Extensions MUST preserve neutral parent meaning, provenance,
language boundaries, and crop-independent interoperability.

## Non-decisions

These families are not enumerations, identifiers, registry partitions, schema
properties, UI facets, or validation categories. Their publication does not
authorize vocabulary population.
