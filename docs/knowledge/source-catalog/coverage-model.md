# Source Coverage Model

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

Coverage is a descriptive statement of what a Source declares it addresses. It
may include Rice Diseases, Rice Insects, Rice Weeds, Growth Stage, IRAC, FRAC,
HRAC, Thai Registration, Plant Anatomy, Plant Physiology, Entomology, or Pathology.

Coverage records domain, subject, geography/jurisdiction, time, population,
document scope, exclusions, and known gaps where available. It is not a taxonomy,
Relationship, Claim, Concept, completeness assertion, or inferred applicability.

Coverage does not imply authoring readiness, scientific authority for every
statement, Evidence sufficiency, currency, rights permission, Package membership,
Diagnosis, or Recommendation. Overlapping coverage creates no equivalence or conflict resolution.
