# Conflict-of-Interest Declaration

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Provide a human declaration and assessment record before review begins.
## Scope
Financial, organizational, authorship, supervisory, competitive, personal,
regulatory, and publication conflicts.
## Out of Scope
This instrument MUST NOT collect unnecessary personal data or decide competence.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KGS-002, KGS-003, and independence policy.
## Definitions
A **conflict** is an interest or relationship that may impair or reasonably appear to impair impartial review.
## Required Inputs
Reviewer identity, assignment, candidate/version, conflict categories, relevant
facts, requested confidentiality, and attestation date.
## Procedure
For each category declare none known, disclosed, or uncertain; explain relevant
facts minimally; submit before access to substantive review; obtain assessment;
record mitigation, recusal, and replacement; update when circumstances change.
## Decision Rules
No declaration means no review. Uncertainty requires assessment. Material
unmitigated conflict requires recusal. A reviewer MUST NOT silently ignore a conflict.
## Responsibilities
Reviewer declares accurately; assessor protects privacy and decides routing; governance resolves disputes.
## Failure Modes
Blank declaration, overcollection, undisclosed authorship, coercion, or retroactive approval.
## Escalation
Disputed assessment goes to independent governance; review remains paused.
## Audit Requirements
Record category outcome, necessary facts, assessor, decision, mitigation, recusal, updates, and retention basis.
## Examples
A reviewer discloses authorship of an earlier fictional draft and is replaced.
## Non-examples
“No problem” without category review MUST NOT pass.
## Change Control
Declaration categories or retention changes require governance and privacy review.
## Future Considerations
Digital forms are not authorized by this document.
