# Reviewer Independence and Recusal

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Protect review judgment from authorship, authority, benefit, pressure, and incompatible duties.
## Scope
Independence assessment, mitigation, recusal, replacement, and re-review.
## Out of Scope
Independence MUST NOT be presumed from role or organization alone.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KGS-002, KGS-003, and KGS-004.
## Definitions
**Recusal** removes a reviewer from decision authority for a stated conflict or competence limitation.
## Required Inputs
Assignment, candidate/version, relationships to authors/sources/outcomes,
competence record, conflict declaration, and proposed mitigation.
## Procedure
Declare before review; assess materiality and appearance; document mitigation;
recuse where impartial judgment could reasonably be impaired; appoint an approved
replacement; transfer only auditable materials; re-review affected decisions when discovered late.
## Decision Rules
Material financial, authorship, supervisory, competitive, personal, regulatory,
organizational, or publication conflict requires recusal unless authorized
governance documents a sufficient lawful mitigation. Silence is never mitigation.
## Responsibilities
Reviewer discloses; Managing Editor assesses initial routing; governance decides contested cases.
## Failure Modes
Self-assessed silence, token second review, informal replacement, or erased prior findings.
## Escalation
Contested independence goes to governance; affected acceptance remains blocked.
## Audit Requirements
Record declaration, assessment, mitigation, recusal, replacement, transferred materials, and re-review.
## Examples
A fictional author recuses from final independent verification of their revision.
## Non-examples
An author approving their own blocker closure MUST NOT pass.
## Change Control
Recusal thresholds require KGS review.
## Future Considerations
Periodic independence audits MAY be governed separately.
