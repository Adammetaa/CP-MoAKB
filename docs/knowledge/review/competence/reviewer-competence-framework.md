# Reviewer Competence Framework

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Ensure each review is assigned to demonstrably competent humans within explicit limits.
## Scope
Nomination, evidence, approval, scope, renewal, suspension, revocation, and records.
## Out of Scope
Employment title, popularity, reviewer ranking, or numeric score MUST NOT establish competence alone.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KGS-002, and KGS-003.
## Definitions
**Competence scope** is an approved combination of subject, task, jurisdiction, and limits.
## Required Inputs
Nominee identity, requested review type, evidence of education or practice,
review experience, recency, jurisdictions, limitations, conflicts, and references where governed.
## Procedure
1. Nominate for a stated scope.
2. Evaluate evidence against the review task.
3. Approve, limit, defer, or reject through authorized governance.
4. Record dimensions: scientific domain, crop, organism group, terminology,
   taxonomy, ontology, evidence methods, regulation, jurisdiction, and publication rights as applicable.
5. Set review/renewal conditions without inventing competence.
6. Renew using current evidence; suspend during investigation or lapse; revoke for
   substantiated loss, misconduct, or authority decision while preserving history.
## Decision Rules
Only approved, current, scope-matched competence permits a decision. Gaps require
co-review, narrower scope, reassignment, or recusal—not informal expansion.
## Responsibilities
Governance authority approves; Managing Editor matches assignments; reviewer reports changes promptly.
## Failure Modes
Title-only approval, expired evidence, implicit jurisdiction, self-expansion, or concealed suspension.
## Escalation
Competence disputes and appeals follow KGS hierarchy with independent assessment.
## Audit Requirements
Record nomination, evidence, assessor, decision, scope, limits, dates, renewal, suspension, and revocation reasons.
## Examples
A fictional terminology reviewer MAY be approved for one language but not scientific taxonomy.
## Non-examples
“Senior” in a job title MUST NOT authorize all review types.
## Change Control
Competence dimensions and approval authority require KGS review.
## Future Considerations
Training MAY support evidence but MUST NOT guarantee approval.
