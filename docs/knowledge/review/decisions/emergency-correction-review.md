# Emergency Correction Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Enable rapid, bounded review of urgent harm or material public error while preserving governance.
## Scope
Emergency correction, withdrawal, retraction, and follow-up review.
## Out of Scope
Urgency MUST NOT waive competence, rights, audit, or retrospective full review.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KGS-001, KGS-003, KGS-005, and KGS-006.
## Definitions
An **emergency** is a documented condition requiring action before the ordinary cycle can complete.
## Required Inputs
Affected identity/version, observed risk/error, evidence, urgency rationale,
proposed minimal action, competent reviewers, conflicts, and recovery plan.
## Procedure
Verify emergency threshold; convene authorized roles; freeze evidence; review the
narrowest correction; record dissent; authorize only the minimum action; notify
affected parties; schedule complete retrospective review.
## Decision Rules
Absent credible urgency uses ordinary review. Unknown rights or scientific scope
MUST remain explicit. Permitted outcomes are correct, withdraw, retract, defer,
escalate, or take no emergency action.
## Responsibilities
Emergency authority decides; competent specialists review; audit custodian preserves evidence.
## Failure Modes
Convenience labeled emergency, broad rewrite, omitted dissent, or no follow-up.
## Escalation
Use KGS emergency and appeal hierarchy without bypassing higher external authority.
## Audit Requirements
Record trigger, evidence, participants, competence, conflicts, action, notices, and retrospective outcome.
## Examples
A fictional published wording inversion MAY receive a minimal correction pending full review.
## Non-examples
Accelerating routine publication MUST NOT qualify.
## Change Control
Emergency powers MAY change only through explicit KGS governance.
## Future Considerations
Exercises MAY test documentation readiness without creating publication actions.
