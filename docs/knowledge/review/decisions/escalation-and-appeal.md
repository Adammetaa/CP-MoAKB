# Escalation and Appeal

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Route unresolved review disputes without erasing original evidence or competence boundaries.
## Scope
Scientific, evidence, terminology, ontology, source-authority, rights, governance, and publication disagreements.
## Out of Scope
Escalation MUST NOT manufacture consensus, override rights, or retry until a preferred result appears.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KGS-001, KGS-003, KGS-004, and KGS-005.
## Definitions
**Escalation** seeks competent resolution; **appeal** challenges a decision on stated grounds; **finality** ends the governed path for that version.
## Required Inputs
Original decision, evidence, dissent, authority, competence records, grounds,
requested remedy, conflicts, and procedural history.
## Procedure
Classify dispute; preserve original record; identify competent authority; notify
affected roles; obtain new conflict declarations; review only stated grounds and
new evidence; issue a reasoned outcome and finality statement.
## Decision Rules
Scientific disputes go to scientific authority; evidence to Evidence Review;
terminology to terminology authority; ontology to ontology authority; source
authority and governance to governance; rights to competent rights authority;
publication to KGS-005 authority. Appeal requires material new evidence,
procedural error, competence error, authority error, or material misapplication.
## Responsibilities
Appellant states grounds; Managing Editor preserves the record; appeal authority remains independent.
## Failure Modes
Forum shopping, erased decision, undisclosed new conflict, scope expansion, or vote overriding competence.
## Escalation
The next authority is determined by KGS hierarchy; rights remain blocked throughout.
## Audit Requirements
Record notice, grounds, evidence, authority, participants, recusals, outcome, reasons, and finality.
## Examples
A fictional evidence appeal MAY reopen after a previously unavailable passage becomes accessible.
## Non-examples
Disagreement alone without grounds MUST NOT erase an adverse decision.
## Change Control
Appeal authority changes require KGS amendment.
## Future Considerations
Future procedures MAY define service targets without changing decision authority.
