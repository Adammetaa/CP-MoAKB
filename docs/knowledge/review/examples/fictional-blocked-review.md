# Fictional Blocked Review

Status: Fictional, illustrative, blocked
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Demonstrate independent blockers that cannot be averaged or voted away.
## Scope
Synthetic candidate `fictional-review-object-nadir`, version 1.
## Out of Scope
Nothing here describes a real crop, pest, disease, chemical, institution, source, diagnosis, or recommendation.
## Authority
Illustrative only under the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md).
## Definitions
**Nadir** is an invented editorial test object with no real-world referent.
## Required Inputs
The submission claims an invented `causes` relationship, cites an inaccessible
*Imaginary Circular N-9*, proposes conflicting invented Thai labels, attaches an
image with no rights statement, and assigns its own author as Scientific Reviewer.
## Procedure
Evidence Review opened an Evidence Gap for missing locator/context. Terminology
Review opened a Conflict for incompatible preferred labels. Ontology and Scientific
Reviews opened a Blocking finding for unsupported causation. Rights Review opened
a Rights Blocker for the image. Governance Review required author-reviewer recusal
and an independent replacement.
## Decision Rules
The Evidence Gap, unsupported causal relationship, Rights Blocker, and unresolved
conflict prohibit acceptance. The conflict of interest pauses the affected review.
No number, confidence percentage, or majority vote may change those effects.
## Responsibilities
Author MUST supply or narrow evidence, withdraw unverified media, answer term
conflict, and accept reassignment. Competent independent reviewers verify closure.
## Failure Modes
Deleting the image without recording the finding, inventing a source passage,
or letting the Author self-close would compound the failure.
## Escalation
Term and scientific disputes follow KGS-004; rights remains blocked; conflict assessment goes to governance.
## Audit Requirements
All findings, rejected responses, recusal, replacement, revisions, and future closure attempts MUST remain.
## Examples
Decision: return for revision and defer scientific decision pending evidence and independent assignment.
## Non-examples
“Four reviewers approve and one blocks, therefore pass” MUST NOT be used.
## Change Control
A resubmission MUST receive a new version and affected reviews.
## Future Considerations
The example remains blocked unless rewritten solely as another fictional version.
