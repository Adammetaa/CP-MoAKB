# Fictional Passing Review

Status: Fictional, illustrative, not published
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Demonstrate a complete review that passes acceptance without creating truth or publication.
## Scope
Synthetic candidate `fictional-review-object-lumen`, version 2.
## Out of Scope
Nothing here describes a real crop, organism, condition, substance, authority, source, diagnosis, or recommendation.
## Authority
Illustrative only under the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md).
## Definitions
**Lumen** is an invented editorial test object with no real-world referent.
## Required Inputs
Candidate identity/version; invented source *Synthetic Review Note L-2* from the
imaginary Neutral Example Office; locator section 1; fictional rights statement;
definition, scope, term, relationship, reviews, and finding log.
## Procedure
Evidence Review verified the invented locator and paraphrase. Scientific Review
confirmed no real assertion. Terminology Review kept the Thai label “วัตถุสมมติลูเมน”
separate from identity. Ontology Review accepted a non-causal `illustrates`
relationship to `fictional-review-context`. Rights Review verified all text was
created for this example. Governance Review checked competence and independence.
## Decision Rules
One Major finding on overbroad scope was revised in version 2 and independently
closed by the originating reviewer. No blocker remains. All decisions apply only to version 2.
## Responsibilities
Different fictional reviewer roles completed Evidence, Scientific, Terminology,
Ontology, Rights, and Governance Reviews; none approved outside competence.
## Failure Modes
Treating the shared word “Lumen” as identity or inferring a causal relationship would fail.
## Escalation
No escalation was required; the original finding remains in the audit history.
## Audit Requirements
The illustration preserves assignments, competence scopes, conflict declarations,
inputs, finding, response, independent closure, decisions, and final gate.
## Examples
Final gate: mandatory reviews complete; blocker count is expressed only as none
unresolved, not a score; rights and traceability pass; acceptance authority recorded.
## Non-examples
The result MUST NOT be described as scientific proof or public release.
## Change Control
Any change to version 2 would require affected reviews again.
## Future Considerations
Publication readiness remains a separate fictional exercise and is not authorized.
