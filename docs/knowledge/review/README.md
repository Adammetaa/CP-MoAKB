# Knowledge Review Framework

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This family provides human-governed review instruments under the
[Knowledge Constitution](../constitution/knowledge-constitution.md),
[Knowledge Authoring Standards](../README.md), [Knowledge Governance
Standards](../governance/README.md), and [Knowledge Editorial
Handbook](../editorial/README.md). It creates no scientific truth, reviewer
ranking, aggregate score, schema, software workflow, acceptance event, or
publication authority.

## Scope

The family covers review types, findings, decisions, competence, independence,
human instruments, and fictional examples.

## Out of Scope

It MUST NOT create content, software, scores, reviewer rankings, acceptance, or publication.

## Authority

The Constitution, KAS, KGS, Editorial Handbook, scoped ADR/RAS authority, Source
Policy, Evidence Levels, Design Freeze, and Publication Boundary remain controlling.

## Definitions

**Family** means this index and its subordinate review documents.

## Required Inputs

Users MUST begin with a fixed candidate version and applicable authority.

Every review MUST identify the exact candidate version, competent reviewer,
inputs, findings, disposition, unresolved issues, and audit record. Completion of
review MUST NOT imply acceptance or publication.

## Decision Rules

Users MUST apply the relevant review type, finding class, and acceptance gate;
they MUST NOT infer authority from navigation order.

## Responsibilities

Authors, reviewers, editors, governance roles, and release roles retain the
responsibilities defined by KGS and the Editorial Handbook.

## Failure Modes

Skipping controlling authority, combining competencies, or treating an index as approval.

## Escalation

Disputes follow KGS-004 and the framework escalation procedure.

## Audit Requirements

Use of an instrument MUST preserve its inputs, decisions, roles, and version.

## Examples

The fictional examples demonstrate a passing and blocked review.

## Non-examples

This index MUST NOT be cited as evidence that a candidate was reviewed.

## Change Control

Family changes require documentation, authority, and verification review.

## Future Considerations

Future instruments MAY be added only without changing higher authority.
