# Review Completion Record

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Prove that one required review was completed properly for one candidate version.
## Scope
Assignment, competence, independence, inputs, findings, response, decision, and closure.
## Out of Scope
Completion MUST NOT imply acceptance, publication readiness, or publication.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), decision-record guidance, and KGS-006.
## Definitions
**Complete** means all mandatory review actions and evidence are recorded for the fixed version.
## Required Inputs
Candidate identity/version, review type, assignment, competence approval, conflict
assessment, reviewed inputs, finding log, revisions, decision, and escalation history.
## Procedure
Verify identity/version; confirm competence and independence; list inputs; reconcile
all findings; identify unresolved issues; record decision and attestation; link audit evidence.
## Decision Rules
Missing assignment, competence, declaration, inputs, findings, reasons, or required
closure means incomplete. Recusal requires a replacement record, not completion.
## Responsibilities
Reviewer attests; Managing Editor verifies completeness; Governance Reviewer may audit but not rewrite findings.
## Failure Modes
Wrong version, copied attestation, open blocker omitted, or completion by recused reviewer.
## Escalation
Incomplete or contested completion returns to reviewer or governance authority.
## Audit Requirements
Retain all referenced records, attestation, verification, corrections, and supersession.
## Examples
A fictional review marked defer can be complete when its evidence and reasons are fully recorded.
## Non-examples
“Review finished” without findings and decision MUST NOT pass.
## Change Control
Completion evidence changes require KGS-006 review.
## Future Considerations
Electronic attestation needs separate governance and implementation approval.
