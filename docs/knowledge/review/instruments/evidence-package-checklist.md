# Evidence Package Checklist

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Confirm that reviewers receive complete, bounded, traceable evidence.
## Scope
Source identity, locators, context, claims, methods, limitations, conflicts, access, and rights.
## Out of Scope
Checklist completion MUST NOT establish sufficiency, truth, or acceptance.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KAS-003/004, and Editorial evidence workflow.
## Definitions
**Pass** means present and reviewable; **N/A** requires a reason; **RR** means revision required.
## Required Inputs
Fixed candidate version and its complete proposed evidence package.
## Procedure
Mark one result per row, cite evidence, explain N/A, and return any Fail or RR before review completion.
## Decision Rules

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Support and contradiction links reach each scoped claim | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |
| Population, method, limitations, date, and jurisdiction are recorded | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |
| Missing, inaccessible, withdrawn, and conflicting evidence is explicit | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |
| Translation, quotation, figure/table, access, and rights handling is reviewed | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |

## Responsibilities
Author assembles; Evidence Reviewer checks fidelity; Rights Reviewer checks permitted use.
## Failure Modes
Citation list without mapping, hidden conflict, excessive quotation, or unjustified N/A.
## Escalation
Evidence meaning goes to Scientific Review; rights defects to Rights Review.
## Audit Requirements
Retain checklist version, rows, notes, evidence links, reviewer, and revision closure.
## Examples
A fictional source with inaccessible appendix records that limitation and cannot pass affected items.
## Non-examples
“Sources attached” MUST NOT substitute for the rows.
## Change Control
Items require KAS and handbook review.
## Future Considerations
This remains a human instrument, not a software form.
