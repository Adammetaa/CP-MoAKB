# Rights and Redistribution Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Verify permitted access, quotation, retention, and redistribution for intended use.
## Scope
Source works, metadata, quotations, images, figures, tables, datasets, and release use.
## Out of Scope
This review is not legal advice and MUST NOT infer permission from access.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), Source Policy,
KAS-004, RAS-015, and Publication Boundary.
## Definitions
A **Rights Blocker** is unknown, absent, conflicting, or insufficient permission for intended use.
## Required Inputs
Source identity/version, intended use, rights statement, jurisdiction, content
type, quotation extent, media inventory, competence, and conflict declaration.
## Review Questions
Who grants which permission for the exact version, content type, jurisdiction,
duration, and intended access, quotation, retention, or redistribution use?
## Mandatory Evidence
The actual rights statement or permission, source/version identity, intended-use
description, media inventory, attribution terms, and restrictions MUST be present.
## Procedure
Verify rights evidence and scope; distinguish access from redistribution; review
each media item separately; document attribution, restrictions, expiry, and alternatives.
## Decision Rules
Unknown redistribution, absent permission, incompatible terms, or unreviewed media
is blocking for that use. Decisions are approve, approve with restrictions,
return, reject, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Competent rights reviewer records evidence; Release Editor enforces blockers but does not invent permission.
## Required Outputs
Rights findings, permitted uses, restrictions, blockers, evidence references,
decision, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Rights-holder affiliation, publication interest, inadequate jurisdiction/rights
competence, or other material conflict requires recusal or governed mitigation.
## Failure Modes
Accessible-equals-reusable, inherited website license, excessive quotation, or silent assumption.
## Escalation
Rights disagreements remain blocking pending competent authority resolution.
## Audit Requirements
Record terms reviewed, version, intended use, finding, restrictions, reviewer, and changes.
## Examples
Metadata MAY pass while redistribution of a fictional original remains blocked.
## Non-examples
A public URL alone MUST NOT prove redistribution permission.
## Change Control
Expanded use requires new review.
## Future Considerations
No automation may waive a Rights Blocker.
