# Publication Readiness Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Assess whether one accepted version is ready for separately governed release authorization.
## Scope
Version, acceptance, specialist completion, rights, traceability, conflicts,
publication boundary, authorization evidence, and rollback.
## Out of Scope
This review MUST NOT publish, tag, release, deploy, upload, or authorize itself.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KGS-005/006,
Editorial publication workflow, and Publication Boundary.
## Definitions
**Publication ready** means eligible for a separate authority decision, not published.
## Required Inputs
Exact accepted version, all review records, closed findings, unresolved-issue
statement, rights findings, traceability, authorization path, and rollback plan.
The reviewer MUST have documented publication-governance and release-boundary competence.
## Review Questions
Is this exact accepted version complete, rights-cleared for intended publication,
traceable, blocker-free, within the Publication Boundary, authorized for the next
decision, and recoverable without rewriting public history?
## Mandatory Evidence
Acceptance record, specialist completion records, closed finding log, rights
decision, traceability evidence, boundary classification, authorization path,
release identity, and rollback plan MUST be present.
## Procedure
Freeze the reviewed version; verify every required approval and closure; inspect
rights, terminology, relationships, governance, boundary, authorization, and rollback evidence.
## Decision Rules
Moving version, incomplete review, unresolved blocker, unknown rights, absent
authorization path, or inadequate rollback blocks readiness. Decisions are ready,
not ready, return, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Release Editor assembles; Governance Reviewer verifies; Project Owner authorization remains separate.
## Required Outputs
Ready/not-ready decision, blocking findings, exact version, evidence references,
unresolved issues, authorization status, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Authorship of the release package, publication interest, inadequate release/right
competence, or another unmitigated conflict requires recusal or independent review.
## Failure Modes
Acceptance treated as publication, technical success used as authority, or conflated release events.
## Escalation
Publication disagreement follows KGS-005; technical action MUST wait.
## Audit Requirements
Record exact version, inputs, findings, decision, authorization, and rollback evidence.
## Examples
An accepted fictional item with verified rights MAY be ready but remains unpublished.
## Non-examples
A clean Git status MUST NOT establish readiness.
## Change Control
Changes require publication-governance review.
## Future Considerations
Automation remains outside scope.
