# Ontology Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Assess conceptual boundaries, epistemic layers, and relationship semantics.
## Scope
Concept distinction, predicate, endpoints, direction, inverse, scope, uncertainty, and risk.
## Out of Scope
Ontology Review MUST NOT define schema, graph behavior, traversal, inference, diagnosis, or recommendation.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), ADR-005, ADR-006, and KAS-006.
## Definitions
**Ontology competence** is approved ability to assess the applicable conceptual model and predicates.
## Required Inputs
Concept identities, relationship candidate, predicate meaning, evidence,
scientific findings, scope, competence, and conflict declaration.
## Review Questions
Are identities and layers distinct, and are predicate meaning, direction, inverse,
endpoints, scope, uncertainty, and risk explicitly supported?
## Mandatory Evidence
Identity records, conceptual authority, predicate definition, scoped relationship
evidence, scientific findings, and high-risk review evidence MUST be present.
## Procedure
Verify layer separation and identities; review predicate, direction, inverse,
scope, evidence, uncertainty, high-risk semantics, and duplicate assertions.
## Decision Rules
Collapsed layers, implicit causation, automatic inverse, unreviewed high-risk
predicate, or ambiguous endpoints is blocking. Permitted decisions are approve,
revise, return, reject, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Ontology Reviewer decides semantics only within approved ontology and domain scope.
## Required Outputs
Semantic findings, relationship disposition, required revisions, unresolved
model questions, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Inadequate conceptual/domain competence, authorship conflict, or commitment to a
competing model that impairs impartiality requires recusal.
## Failure Modes
Physical-model prescription, inferred relationship, label endpoints, or hidden conflict.
## Escalation
Semantic disputes follow KGS-004; scientific meaning returns to Scientific Review.
## Audit Requirements
Record model/predicate authority, inputs, findings, decision, dissent, and closure.
## Examples
A fictional non-causal relationship MAY pass with explicit scope.
## Non-examples
Co-occurrence converted to `causes` MUST NOT pass.
## Change Control
Conceptual changes require ADR and KAS impact review.
## Future Considerations
Implementation mapping requires separate architecture approval.
