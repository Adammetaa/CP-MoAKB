# Terminology Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Assess language-specific term meaning, status, scope, and ambiguity.
## Scope
Preferred, alternative, scientific, common, local, historical, deprecated,
transliterated, translated, ambiguous, and trade terms.
## Out of Scope
Terminology Review MUST NOT create identity, scientific truth, or UI authority.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KAS-005, ADR-006, and ADR-007.
## Definitions
**Term status** is a governed disposition for one language and scope, not identity.
## Required Inputs
Candidate identity, proposed terms, languages, contexts, sources, uncertainty,
scientific-name review where applicable, competence, and conflict declaration.
## Review Questions
Is each term linked to identity without becoming identity, supported for its
language/type/scope, and explicit about translation, ambiguity, and local context?
## Mandatory Evidence
Usage sources, language and locale, term type, scope, translation/transliteration
notes, ambiguity evidence, and applicable scientific-name authority MUST be present.
## Procedure
Verify identity separation; inspect usage evidence; distinguish translation from
transliteration; test ambiguity and local context; record term-specific findings.
## Decision Rules
Label-as-identity, automatic equivalence, unsupported preferred status, or hidden
ambiguity blocks terminology acceptance. Decisions are approve, revise, return,
reject, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Terminology Reviewer acts only in documented language/domain scope and refers scientific names appropriately.
## Required Outputs
Term-specific findings, scoped status decision, uncertainty, required revisions,
dissent, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Inadequate language/domain competence, authorship without required independence,
or material organizational, personal, or commercial interest requires recusal.
## Failure Modes
Spelling-based merge, UI translation promoted to authority, or universalized local usage.
## Escalation
Terminology disagreement follows KGS-004; identity disputes go to identity governance.
## Audit Requirements
Record languages, competence, sources, term findings, status, dissent, and history.
## Examples
An uncertain fictional Thai label MAY be deferred explicitly.
## Non-examples
Machine translation alone MUST NOT establish equivalence.
## Change Control
Meaning or status changes require renewed terminology review.
## Future Considerations
Vocabulary implementation remains separately governed.
