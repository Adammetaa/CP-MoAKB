# Governance Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Verify authority, role, process, lifecycle, conflict, and audit compliance.
## Scope
Constitution, KAS, KGS, scoped ADR/RAS boundaries, exceptions, and decisions.
## Out of Scope
Governance Review MUST NOT decide scientific truth or rights by vote.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), Constitution, and KGS-001 through KGS-006.
## Definitions
A **Governance Blocker** is a material violation of applicable authority or decision procedure.
## Required Inputs
Fixed candidate, all specialist reviews, competence and conflict records,
finding log, lifecycle history, authority versions, and requested decision.
## Review Questions
Were the correct authorities, roles, competence scopes, review gates, conflict
controls, lifecycle rules, exceptions, appeals, and audit duties followed?
## Mandatory Evidence
Authority versions, appointments, competence and conflict records, specialist
completion records, finding log, lifecycle history, and decision request MUST be present.
## Procedure
Verify controlling authority, appointments, competence scope, independence,
required review completion, finding closure, exceptions, appeals, and audit integrity.
## Decision Rules
Missing authority, unauthorized decision, undisclosed conflict, unresolved blocker,
or incomplete mandatory review blocks acceptance. Decisions are approve, revise,
return, reject, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Governance Reviewer MUST identify authority and MUST NOT substitute for specialist review.
## Required Outputs
Compliance findings, authority map, blocker disposition, reasoned decision,
escalation path, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Participation in the contested decision, supervisory conflict, governance-interest
conflict, or insufficient authority competence requires recusal.
## Failure Modes
Invented exception, authority laundering, majority override, or acceptance/publication conflation.
## Escalation
Authority and governance disputes follow KGS-001/004 and applicable appeal authority.
## Audit Requirements
Record authorities, questions, findings, exceptions, decisions, appeals, and closure.
## Examples
A complete package MAY still be deferred for an unresolved scientific blocker.
## Non-examples
Passing tests MUST NOT count as governance approval.
## Change Control
This review MUST NOT amend its governing documents.
## Future Considerations
Recurring issues MAY support a separately authorized governance amendment.
