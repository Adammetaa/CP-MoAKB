# Scientific Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Assess scientific meaning, support, limitations, uncertainty, and neutrality.
## Scope
Scoped claims, definitions, causal assertions, methods, populations, and conflicts.
## Out of Scope
Scientific Review MUST NOT decide rights, terminology authority, governance, diagnosis, or advice.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KAS-001, KAS-003, and KGS-002/003.
## Definitions
**Scientific competence** is documented expertise relevant to the exact claim scope.
## Required Inputs
Fixed candidate version, evidence package, scopes, limitations, adverse evidence,
review questions, competence approval, and conflict declaration.
## Review Questions
Does each claim match its evidence, population, method, scope, date, jurisdiction,
limitations, uncertainty, and credible contradiction without diagnosis or advice?
## Mandatory Evidence
Claim-linked evidence, adverse evidence, source context, method and limitation notes,
and the approved competence and conflict records MUST be present.
## Procedure
Confirm competence; test claim/evidence fit, method, population, date, jurisdiction,
uncertainty, disagreement, and overstatement; record every finding and disposition.
## Decision Rules
Unsupported certainty, material scope mismatch, unreviewed causation, or unresolved
competent scientific conflict is blocking. Decisions are approve, approve with
required revision, return, reject, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Reviewer MUST remain neutral, cite reviewed evidence, and recuse for inadequate competence or independence.
## Required Outputs
Finding log, evidence references, decision with reasons, required revisions,
unresolved issues, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Insufficient domain scope, authorship, material interest, supervisory pressure, or
another unmitigated conflict requires recusal and replacement.
## Failure Modes
Authority by job title, majority override, omitted adverse evidence, or recommendation language.
## Escalation
Scientific disagreement follows KGS-004 and MUST preserve both positions.
## Audit Requirements
Record competence, questions, inputs, evidence, findings, decision, dissent, and closure.
## Examples
A fictional claim limited to its invented evidence scope MAY pass.
## Non-examples
“All reviewers agree” without evidence MUST NOT pass.
## Change Control
Material changes require scientific and governance review.
## Future Considerations
No automated scientific scoring is authorized.
