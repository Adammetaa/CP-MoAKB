# Evidence Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Verify source-to-evidence and evidence-to-claim fidelity and traceability.
## Scope
Identity, version, locator, context, methods, limitations, contradiction, and withdrawal.
## Out of Scope
Evidence Review MUST NOT score truth or substitute for scientific or rights review.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [framework](../knowledge-review-framework.md), KAS-003, KAS-004, and Source Policy.
## Definitions
**Traceability** is a reversible path among source, evidence item, and scoped claim.
## Required Inputs
Source candidate, evidence item, candidate claim, locators, extraction notes,
rights disposition, and access status.
## Review Questions
Is the exact source identifiable and accessible, is the extraction faithful and
bounded, and can every evidence item and claim be traced in both directions?
## Mandatory Evidence
Source identity/version, locators, source context, extraction notes, claim links,
limitations, adverse evidence, and access/withdrawal status MUST be present.
## Procedure
Verify identity and version; inspect passage and context; compare paraphrase;
check support and contradiction links; record inaccessible, withdrawn, or missing context.
## Decision Rules
Missing identity, unverifiable locator, material distortion, silent harmonization,
or absent claim link is blocking. Permitted decisions are approve, revise, return,
reject, defer, escalate, or recuse.
## Responsibilities
Evidence Reviewer verifies fidelity within methods competence and refers scientific meaning separately.
## Required Outputs
Traceability findings, corrected mappings where required, decision, unresolved
access issues, and completion record.
## Recusal Conditions
Authorship of the extraction does not automatically disqualify review, but absence
of independent verification where required, inadequate methods competence, or
material conflict requires recusal.
## Failure Modes
Citation-only approval, excessive copying, context stripping, or unsupported extrapolation.
## Escalation
Interpretation disputes go to Scientific Review; source-authority disputes follow KGS-004.
## Audit Requirements
Record source version, passages inspected, findings, corrections, decision, and closure verification.
## Examples
A precise fictional locator and bounded paraphrase MAY pass.
## Non-examples
A bibliography entry without evidence mapping MUST NOT pass.
## Change Control
Changes MUST preserve KAS-003/004 semantics.
## Future Considerations
Machine extraction is outside this framework.
