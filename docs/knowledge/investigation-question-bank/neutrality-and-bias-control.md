# Neutrality and Bias Control

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Core Rules

1. A question must not assume a cause or Diagnosis.
2. A question must not imply a preferred Hypothesis Candidate.
3. A question must not imply treatment need, pesticide selection, or Recommendation.
4. A question must not assume severity, resistance, identity, or progression.
5. Local terminology must not be treated as confirmed scientific identity.
6. Absence of a record must not become absence of a condition or feature.
7. Wording must not lead the respondent toward a predetermined answer.
8. Missing, unknown, unavailable, conflicting, and not-applicable responses remain available.
9. Pattern order, emphasis, and follow-up relationships must not communicate rank.
10. Translation and contextual substitution must preserve the reviewed neutrality.

## Wording Guidance

Prefer open wording when the response space is not safely bounded. Use
bounded-neutral wording when the possible response forms are explicit and
include uncertainty. Name the observed feature or requested context rather than
a presumed causal or diagnostic label. Ask what, where, when, how, or on what
basis without embedding the expected answer.

## Bias Tests

Reviewers ask whether the wording:

- would still make sense if every considered hypothesis were false;
- gives competing explanations an equal opportunity to be challenged;
- embeds an unverified scientific identity or causal vocabulary;
- turns a missing record into a negative answer;
- frames one response as desirable, expert, compliant, or expected;
- uses a confirmation tag such as “correct?” after an assertion; or
- could cause interface presentation to act as diagnosis or recommendation.

A failed pattern returns for revision or rejection.

## Acceptable Architecture Illustrations

- On which plant structure was the feature observed?
- Where on the leaf did the feature first become visible?
- Was the feature directly observed, reported, or measured?
- How was the pattern distributed within the observed area?
- Was a specimen captured?
- Is the requested information unknown, not recorded, unavailable, or not applicable?

## Prohibited Architecture Illustrations

- Is this blast disease?
- Did stem borer cause the dead heart?
- Which pesticide should be applied?
- Is the insect resistant to Group 4A?
- How severe is the confirmed bacterial infection?
- The symptom started at the leaf tip, correct?

The prohibited forms presuppose diagnosis, causal identity, treatment,
resistance, severity, or a preferred answer. They must not be repaired merely by
adding an uncertainty option.

## Review and Conflict

Neutrality review records the wording examined, language, context, rationale,
reviewer, dissent, and limitations. Disagreement remains explicit. Approval does
not authorize automated selection, publication, or use in every context.
