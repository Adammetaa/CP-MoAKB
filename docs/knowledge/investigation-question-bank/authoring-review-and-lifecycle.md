# Authoring, Review, and Lifecycle

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Human-governed Workflow

### 1. Nomination

A nominator states the proposed investigative need, intended users and
languages, source or editorial basis, and why reuse is justified.

### 2. Intent Classification

An author selects and explains the Question Intent without implying a diagnosis,
answer, sequence, or priority.

### 3. Pattern Drafting

The author drafts neutral wording guidance, Question Scope, applicability,
trigger and exclusion considerations, response expectation, missing-state
allowances, Evidence Need Reference, and relevant follow-up meanings.

### 4. Neutrality Review

A reviewer applies [Neutrality and Bias Control](neutrality-and-bias-control.md)
in every supported language. Leading or inferential patterns return for revision
or rejection.

### 5. Applicability Review

A reviewer checks contextual relevance, exclusions, duplication, safety,
authority, and whether any condition has become executable selection logic.

### 6. Response-expectation Review

A reviewer verifies response type, required context, units, uncertainty, and
explicit missing-information options without false certainty.

### 7. Terminology Review

A qualified reviewer assesses labels, definitions, ambiguity, translation,
transliteration, local expressions, and scientific identity boundaries under KAS-005.

### 8. Scientific Review Where Applicable

Specialist review examines scientific terminology, methods, measurements, or
context where relevant. It does not turn the pattern into diagnosis content.

### 9. Approval

Authorized governance records approval, revision, or rejection with rationale,
scope, reviewers, conflicts, and limitations. Approval does not create a dataset
record, question instance, or publication.

### 10. Publication Eligibility

Eligibility separately confirms authority, provenance, required reviews,
language quality, licensing or attribution duties, version history, and
Publication Boundary. Eligibility is not publication.

### 11. Revision, Deprecation, Replacement, or Withdrawal

Material revisions repeat affected reviews. Retirement actions preserve history,
rationale, references, and replacement meaning without silently rewriting uses.

## Lifecycle States

- **proposed:** nominated but not accepted for review outcome;
- **under review:** undergoing one or more required human reviews;
- **approved:** accepted by authorized governance for its stated purpose;
- **publication eligible:** separately determined ready to be considered for publication;
- **deprecated:** retained but discouraged for new use with rationale;
- **replaced:** superseded by an explicitly related reviewed pattern;
- **withdrawn:** removed from eligible use by an authorized decision;
- **rejected:** not accepted, with failed criteria and rationale preserved.

States are not an automatic sequence. Approved does not imply publication
eligible, published, selected, or instantiated. Replacement does not erase prior history.

## Audit and Change Control

Governance preserves nomination, drafts, sources, language versions, review
comments, conflicts, decisions, conditions, versions, and retirement history.
Appeals identify the contested decision and new or overlooked basis; they do not
bypass review. Tools may support traceability but never make lifecycle decisions.
