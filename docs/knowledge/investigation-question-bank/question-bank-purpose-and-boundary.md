# Question Bank Purpose and Boundary

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

The Question Bank provides governed patterns from which a human may author a
neutral Investigation Question for a bounded Information Gap. Reuse improves
consistency, explainability, localization, and review without replacing judgment.

## Inclusion Boundary

The architecture may organize Question Intents, scopes, reusable wording
patterns, applicability and exclusion considerations, possible-use triggers,
response expectations, Evidence Need References, follow-up meanings, review
states, and lifecycle states. Each pattern must disclose the investigation need
it can help address and the contexts in which it may mislead or duplicate work.

## Exclusion Boundary

A Question Pattern is not a rendered question instance, Observation,
Information Gap, Evidence Need, Evidence Object, Claim, Hypothesis, Diagnosis,
or Recommendation. The Question Bank does not select patterns, populate
questions, collect responses, interpret answers, branch a workflow, score
hypotheses, or prescribe action.

## Pattern Versus Instance

A pattern expresses reusable semantic and wording guidance. A question instance
is a separately authored use of a pattern for a particular case, gap, language,
scope, and context. Approval of a pattern does not approve every instance.
Rendering, translation, substitution, and contextualization require review and
must not introduce assumptions absent from the pattern.

## Investigation Boundary

An Information Gap may motivate consideration of a pattern, but the gap does
not automatically trigger or contain the question. A response to a question may
inform investigation, but it does not automatically become an Observation or
Evidence. An Evidence Need Reference points to the kind of separately governed
need that may be relevant; it is not an Evidence Object.

## Localization and Extension

Thai-first patterns may preserve Thai wording, locale, translation provenance,
and independently reviewed English technical alignment. Translation must retain
intent, neutrality, uncertainty choices, and scope. Local terminology must not
be treated as confirmed scientific identity. Domain extensions may introduce
reviewed terminology without embedding any crop in the core architecture.

## Future Workflow Compatibility

Future SPA and Agronomist experiences may help humans browse, filter, render,
answer, and review patterns. Such capabilities require separate implementation
authority. Interface order, defaults, completion indicators, or prior responses
must not automatically select a question or create a decision tree.
