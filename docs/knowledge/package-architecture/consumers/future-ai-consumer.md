# Future AI Consumer Boundary

Future AI may consume approved CKP representations, provenance-aware chunks,
citation metadata, exact versions, Review State, unresolved issues, authority
scope, and limitations only under separate architecture and validation.

An unreviewed Candidate, chat transcript, generated answer, model confidence, UI
copy, or previous AI output MUST NOT be canonical Knowledge or Evidence. AI output
remains Generated Interpretation unless separately promoted through governed
authoring and review. This document implements no AI or RAG.
