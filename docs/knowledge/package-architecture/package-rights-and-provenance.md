# Package Rights and Provenance

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

Source copyright and reuse of independently authored facts or Claims remain
separate assessments. A supporting Source does not authorize redistribution of
its full text, images, tables, or other protected expression.

Where permitted, Source Evidence may support governed extraction. A public
Representation may include citation, provenance, a bounded lawful quotation, and
an independently authored factual summary. CKPs MUST NOT copy full copyrighted
works into Package assets.

Rights status, license/permission, jurisdiction, allowed uses, attribution,
restrictions, expiry, and review evidence remain traceable by exact Source and
Representation version. Rights uncertainty blocks incompatible consumer use.
