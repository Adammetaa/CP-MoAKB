# Disease Package Profile

Conceptual coverage may reference a canonical Disease Concept; pathogen/cause
Concepts; terminology; crop host; plant organ; growth-stage relevance;
Observation Vocabulary; symptom-like/sign-like observations; damage patterns;
mechanism and epidemiology/environment Claims; differential Relationships;
supporting and contradicting Evidence; Management categories; Active Ingredients;
FRAC Relationships where applicable; resistance and regulatory limitations;
unresolved issues; follow-up requirements; Source authority; reviews; and
publication status.

Membership of Management or Active Ingredient knowledge is never an automatic
Recommendation, and Evidence support is never Confirmed Diagnosis.
