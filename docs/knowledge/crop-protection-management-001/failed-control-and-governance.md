# Failed-control Investigation and Governance

`IQ-CPM-001/v1` through `IQ-CPM-016/v1` ask, with domain relevance and no score:

1. Was the target correctly identified?
2. What life stage or weed growth stage was present?
3. What Active Ingredient was actually used?
4. What formulation and concentration were on the label?
5. What labeled rate was used? (Never solicit or authorize exceeding it.)
6. When was the application made?
7. Was spray coverage adequate and uniform?
8. What water volume and application method were used?
9. What weather occurred before and after application?
10. What was the water condition in the rice field?
11. Did rainfall occur in the relevant interval?
12. Could reinfestation or later emergence explain the observation?
13. Was the same authority-specific MoA repeatedly used?
14. Did other target organisms respond differently?
15. Was the product, registration and exact crop/target/use pattern valid then?
16. Is resistance only a plausible Hypothesis requiring competent escalation?

The deterministic flow is `missing information -> application/identity/timing/
environment/reinfestation checks -> MoA information -> next investigation step ->
human review`. No numerical resistance score, Diagnosis, treatment selection,
rotation program, dose escalation, Decision or Recommendation is generated.
