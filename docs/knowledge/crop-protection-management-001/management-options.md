# Source-backed Management Options

Management Options `MO-CPM-001/v1` through `MO-CPM-007/v1` use the existing
Management Option Package Profile. They describe available evidence categories;
they do not select, prioritize, decide, or recommend an action.

| Option | Domain / linked problem CKPs | Evidence | Bounded description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `MO-CPM-001/v1` Monitoring | Insect; 19 `CKP-RIC-*` packages | `EV-RIC-001/v1`-`EV-RIC-019/v1` exact subject locators | inspect source-supported life stage, plant part, damage pattern, field distribution and rice stage before management consideration |
| `MO-CPM-002/v1` Natural-enemy monitoring | Insect; relevant `CKP-RIC-*` packages | `RL-RIC-NE-001/v1`-`RL-RIC-NE-010/v1`; RD printed pp.71-88 / PDF pp.82-99 | observe source-named predators/parasitoids; presence does not guarantee control |

## Disease boundary

The 16 disease CKPs remain connected to the management view with status
`Management evidence required`. Existing disease Evidence supports identity,
symptoms, causal agents, spread and field context, but the accepted corpus
explicitly deferred chemical efficacy, current registration, FRAC and treatment.
No disease Management Option is inferred from a spread pathway.

## Historical chemical context

`CL-CPM-HIST-001/v1` references `EV-RWC-006/v1` and `EV-RWC-007/v1`: the fixed
2019 manual describes herbicide families, application classes and mode-of-action
categories in its training scope. This is historical/source-contextual Knowledge,
not current HRAC classification, label permission, efficacy or Recommendation.
