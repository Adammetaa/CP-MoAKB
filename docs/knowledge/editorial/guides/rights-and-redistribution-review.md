# Guide: Rights and Redistribution Review

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Prevent access from being mistaken for permission to redistribute.
## Scope
Metadata, checksums, quotations, original publications, images, figures, and tables.
## Out of Scope
This guide is not legal advice and MUST NOT infer a license.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [handbook](../knowledge-editorial-handbook.md), [Source Policy](../../../SOURCE_POLICY.md), and [Publication Boundary](../../../release/publication-boundary.md).
## Definitions
**Access** permits viewing; **redistribution** republishes content; **metadata** identifies a work without reproducing it.
## Responsibilities
Author identifies intended use; rights reviewer verifies terms; Release Editor enforces blockers.
## Procedure
Retain necessary source metadata and checksum where permitted; record access and
redistribution separately; verify an explicit license or permission; keep
quotations limited and attributed; perform separate review for every image,
figure, and table; link to official sources when reproduction is not verified.
## Required Inputs
Source identity, intended use, rights statement, jurisdiction, and content type.
## Required Outputs
Rights finding, evidence, permitted use, restrictions, reviewer, and blocker status.
## Review Points
Version, rights holder statement, quotation extent, media rights, attribution, and expiry.
## Failure Modes
Accessible-equals-reusable, inherited website license, excessive quote, or unreviewed image.
## Examples
Metadata and a checksum MAY be retained while the fictional original remains excluded.
## Non-examples
Uploading a found publication without verified rights MUST NOT pass.
## Escalation
Unknown or conflicting rights remain blockers and go to competent rights/governance review.
## Audit Requirements
Retain reviewed terms, retrieval evidence, decision, restrictions, and later changes.
## Change Control
Any redistribution expansion requires new verified authority.
## Future Considerations
No publication convenience may weaken rights review.
