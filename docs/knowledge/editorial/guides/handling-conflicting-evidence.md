# Guide: Handling Conflicting Evidence

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Preserve and review substantive disagreement without forced consensus.
## Scope
Source-specific support, contradiction, review, and unresolved disposition.
## Out of Scope
Editors MUST NOT vote scientific truth or silently harmonize sources.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [handbook](../knowledge-editorial-handbook.md), KAS-003, and KGS-004.
## Definitions
A **conflict** is a material disagreement within overlapping scope; **unresolved** is a valid explicit state.
## Responsibilities
Author records both sides; reviewers test comparability; governance manages escalation.
## Procedure
Separate source-specific claims; compare population, method, date, jurisdiction,
definitions, and limitations; request competent review; record agreement and
remaining disagreement; retain unresolved status when evidence cannot resolve it.
## Required Inputs
All relevant evidence items, scopes, methods, limitations, and prior decisions.
## Required Outputs
Conflict statement, comparison, reviewer findings, disposition, and open questions.
## Review Points
Completeness, comparable scope, adverse evidence, competence, and neutrality.
## Failure Modes
Cherry-picking, majority counting, source ranking without scope, or erased dissent.
## Examples
Two fictional sources MAY remain as contradictory scoped claims.
## Non-examples
Selecting the convenient source without reasons MUST NOT pass.
## Escalation
Follow KGS-004 and preserve the record during appeal.
## Audit Requirements
Retain evidence, comparison, reviewers, recusals, decisions, dissent, and appeals.
## Change Control
Resolved status requires new evidence or an authorized reasoned decision.
## Future Considerations
No automated truth scoring is authorized.
