# Guide: Corrections, Deprecation, and Supersession

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Apply distinct lifecycle actions while preserving history.
## Scope
Corrections, deprecation, supersession, retirement, retraction, and withdrawal.
## Out of Scope
These actions MUST NOT silently rewrite published identity or erase evidence.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [handbook](../knowledge-editorial-handbook.md), KAS-007, KGS-003, KGS-005, and KGS-006.
## Definitions
**Correction** fixes error while preserving identity where authorized;
**deprecation** discourages future use; **supersession** names a successor;
**retirement** ends active use; **retraction** withdraws validity; **withdrawal** removes a release from active distribution under authority.
## Responsibilities
Author reports; Managing Editor scopes; reviewers assess; governance decides;
Release Editor handles publication consequences only when authorized.
## Procedure
Identify affected identity and version; preserve prior state; classify the action;
state reason, evidence, impact, effective point, successor if any, and notice;
obtain required reviews; update references without reusing identity.
## Required Inputs
Current record, issue report, evidence, impact analysis, and authority.
## Required Outputs
Decision, reason, lifecycle action, successor link, notices, and audit history.
## Review Points
Classification, identity continuity, evidence, affected releases, conflicts, and communication.
## Failure Modes
Silent edit, identifier reuse, deprecation without reason, or supersession without successor.
## Examples
A fictional wording error MAY be corrected with unchanged meaning and recorded history.
## Non-examples
Deleting a disputed record to hide disagreement MUST NOT pass.
## Escalation
Material disputes and appeals follow KGS-004; emergency correction follows KGS-001/005.
## Audit Requirements
Retain every version, reason, decision, actor role, effective point, and notice.
## Change Control
Lifecycle semantics require KAS/KGS approval.
## Future Considerations
Implementation migration remains a separate engineering scope.
