# Scientific Reviewer Checklist

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Record competent scientific review without deciding other specialties.
## Scope
Scientific meaning, support, limitations, neutrality, and conflicts.
## Out of Scope
The reviewer MUST NOT diagnose, recommend, decide rights, or approve outside competence.
## Authority
Use under the [handbook](../knowledge-editorial-handbook.md), KGS-002, and KGS-003.
## Definitions
**RR** means revision required; **N/A** requires a reason.
## Responsibilities
Reviewer declares competence and conflicts, then completes every row.
## Procedure
Mark one result per row; explain Fail, N/A, and RR; select an allowed review decision.
## Required Inputs
Candidate, evidence, scope, conflicts, and review brief.
## Required Outputs
Checklist, decision, reasons, and revisions.

## Failure Modes
Authority by title, scope overreach, hidden dissent, or substituting for terminology/rights review.
## Examples
A reviewer MAY defer beyond-competence taxonomy.
## Non-examples
Approval without reviewing adverse evidence MUST NOT pass.
## Escalation
Recuse or escalate under KGS-003/004.
## Audit Requirements
Retain competence, disclosure, findings, decision, and dissent.
## Change Control
Role authority MUST NOT be expanded through this checklist.
## Future Considerations
Domain-specific questions require separate governance; this is not a software form.
