# Governance Reviewer Checklist

Status: Active
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Verify authority, process, conflict, accountability, and audit compliance.
## Scope
Constitution, KAS, KGS, ADR/RAS boundaries, roles, lifecycle, and conflicts.
## Out of Scope
Governance review MUST NOT decide scientific truth by vote.
## Authority
Use under the [handbook](../knowledge-editorial-handbook.md), Constitution, and KGS-001 through KGS-006.
## Definitions
**RR** means revision required; **N/A** requires a reason.
## Responsibilities
Governance Reviewer confirms authority scope and remains independent.
## Procedure
Mark one result per row; identify controlling authority for every blocker.
## Required Inputs
Candidate, reviews, disclosures, decisions, conflicts, and authority versions.
## Required Outputs
Checklist, compliance disposition, blockers, and escalation path.
## Review Points
| Review item | Pass | Fail | N/A | RR | Reviewer notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Higher authorities and scoped ADR/RAS boundaries are preserved | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |
| Roles acted only within competence and conflicts were disclosed | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |

| Disagreement and unknown states remain visible | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |
| Acceptance remains separate from publication authorization | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | |
## Failure Modes
Invented authority, silent exception, missing recusal, or premature publication claim.
## Examples
A compliant package MAY still remain scientifically unresolved.
## Non-examples
Passing technical tests treated as governance approval MUST NOT pass.
## Escalation
Use the hierarchy, appeal, and emergency paths in KGS-001/004/005.
## Audit Requirements
Retain authorities, findings, decisions, exceptions, appeals, and reviewer role.
## Change Control
This instrument MUST NOT amend its governing authorities.
## Future Considerations
Exception patterns MAY inform a separately approved KGS amendment; this is not a software form.
