# Knowledge Lab Design Principles

Status: Active blueprint
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Define product principles for every future Lab screen and interaction.
## Scope
Evidence, review, status, accessibility, localization, and governance UX.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [primary blueprint](../knowledge-workspace-blueprint.md).
## Audience
Product, UX, content, accessibility, and governance reviewers.
## User Goals
Understand meaning, provenance, responsibility, uncertainty, disagreement, and next permitted action.
## Information Shown
Evidence before knowledge; traceability and explainability by design; version,
competence, rights, conflict, and publication state at the point of decision.
## Actions
Use progressive disclosure, comparison, explicit handoff, clear empty states, and boundary notices.
## Prohibited Actions
No silent inference, automatic publication, reviewer leaderboard, confidence score,
color-only status, hidden disagreement, or automated scientific priority.
## Workflow
Human review authority and separation of duties govern every action path.
## Failure Modes
Convenient UI that collapses unknown states, roles, evidence layers, or lifecycle gates.
## Empty States
State why information is absent: Unknown, Unavailable, Not reviewed, Out of scope, or Not applicable.
## Accessibility
Text labels, icons/shapes, descriptions, logical headings, keyboard support,
responsive reflow, readable Thai type, and precise English technical terms.
## Governance Boundaries
The future UI SHOULD be Thai-first, bilingual Thai/English, scientifically
precise, and consistent with the Explorer localization policy. Thai-first
bilingual UI MUST preserve English scientific names, standards,
identifiers, lifecycle/schema version identifiers, IRAC, FRAC, HRAC, and BBCH where applicable.
UI localization MUST NOT establish accepted terminology.
## Audit Requirements
Design decisions affecting authority, status, or safety warnings require recorded review.
## Examples
An operational “human priority: urgent” label states it is not scientific importance.
## Non-examples
A red badge alone representing a Rights Blocker MUST NOT be used.
## Future Implementation Considerations
Static-prototype compatibility is required; localization implementation is deferred.
## Change Control
Principle changes require product, terminology, accessibility, and governance review.
