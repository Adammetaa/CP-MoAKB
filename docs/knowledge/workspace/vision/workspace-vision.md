# Workspace Vision

Status: Active blueprint
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
State why the Knowledge Lab exists and what experience it must provide.
## Scope
Human authoring, independent review, traceability, disagreement, and governed preparation.
## Out of Scope
Automatic generation, autonomous review, AI editing, diagnosis, recommendation, and publication authority.
## Authority
Subordinate to the [primary blueprint](../knowledge-workspace-blueprint.md) and its authorities.
## Audience
Knowledge contributors, reviewers, product designers, and governors.
## User Goals
See evidence before claims; understand candidate status; work within competence;
preserve dissent; explain decisions; prepare a complete governed handoff.
## Information Shown
Traceability chains, versions, roles, open findings, rights, uncertainty, decisions, and history.
## Actions
Support deliberate authoring, review requests, findings, revision, comparison, handoff, and archival inspection.
## Prohibited Actions
Never generate knowledge, decide truth, resolve conflict by merge, rank reviewers, or publish automatically.
## Workflow
Work progresses through visible human gates with explicit inputs, outputs, blockers, and accountable roles.
## Failure Modes
Fast workflow that hides uncertainty, generic approval outside competence, or product language that implies certainty.
## Empty States
Explain missing assignments, evidence, or candidate work and link to guidance rather than fabricate suggestions.
## Accessibility
Thai-first future copy, bilingual support, plain-language boundaries, keyboard operability, and non-color status cues.
## Governance Boundaries
Review independence and publication separation are product requirements, not optional interface settings.
## Audit Requirements
Every meaningful action and role transition must remain attributable and version-aware.
## Examples
A Rights Blocker remains prominent even when other reviews are complete.
## Non-examples
A progress ring labeled “knowledge quality 90%” is prohibited.
## Future Implementation Considerations
Prototype usability research SHOULD test comprehension of authority and uncertainty without real content.
## Change Control
Vision changes require owner, knowledge-governance, product, and accessibility review.
