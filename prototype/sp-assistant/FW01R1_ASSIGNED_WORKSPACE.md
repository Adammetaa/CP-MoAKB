# FW01R1 assigned SPA workspace reachability

The primary Field listing now separates owned Fields from server-returned assigned Fields and Cases. Assigned data stays in memory; it is not imported into the owner's browser-local workspace repository. The owner detail retains its existing local-first services. Assigned Field Detail uses the same product route and visual shell but renders from a fresh scoped server projection, because the owner-only local service must not be made authoritative for another user's Field.

`FIELD_OPERATE` enables Field discovery, reviewed Field state, permitted Cases, and Case creation or resumption. `CASE_OPERATE` without a Field grant appears only in the assigned Case list. `CASE_REVIEW` appears there as read/review only, with no investigation or image-capture controls. `REVIEW_ITEM_REVIEW` stays on the existing Learning Inbox surface and does not create Field or Case access. The browser renders the server's `permissions` object; it does not infer these capabilities from the SPA role.

Every assigned Case read is constrained to one Case. The Case bundle supplies only the Field identity, crop/season, and stage needed for that investigation; Field History, unrelated Cases, and unrelated attachments are not included. Assigned text and image capture reuse the existing governed conversation and visual-evidence runtimes under the record owner's data scope, with a per-request operational audit entry identifying the acting user. A photo stays `VE0_RAW_IMAGE_ONLY` until the established quality and human review gates act on it. The scoped API rechecks the active grant on every open, turn, image upload, or review; browser navigation and hidden controls are not security boundaries.

No schema or scientific rule changes are introduced. Existing Case, Observation, Evidence, conversation, review, and Learning Inbox authorities remain in place. The assigned Case surface intentionally omits owner-only Field metadata editing, Field-wide history for Case-only users, and unrelated follow-up or management controls. It is not a new investigation model or a claim of real-field validation.
